## What and why

Orders take a fourth self-trade prevention mode, `DECREMENT`
(`SelfTradePreventionMode` value 4). When an incoming order in `DECREMENT`
would trade against a resting order of the same root account, including
another subaccount of that root, neither trades. Instead, both orders are
reduced by the quantity that would have traded:

- The order left with no quantity is canceled with the new cancel reason
  `CANCEL_REASON_STP_DECREMENT` (value 19). When the two quantities are
  equal, both are canceled. Fills before the cancel stand.
- A resting order that keeps a quantity keeps its place in the queue. Its
  quantity is lowered by what self-trade prevention removed, and the funds
  reserved for that part are released at once.
- An incoming order that keeps a quantity goes on matching other accounts'
  orders. Its quantity is lowered too, and it may rest with what is left,
  reserving only that.
- A fill-or-kill order that would meet such an order before it is completely
  filled is rejected `FOK_INSUFFICIENT_LIQUIDITY` instead of reduced.

The incoming order's mode decides; a resting order's own mode does not
matter. `DECREMENT` is accepted for orders without attached take-profit,
stop-loss or trailing-stop legs. An order with attached legs, a modify that
adds legs to a `DECREMENT` order, and every trigger create are rejected
with `ERROR_CODE_STP_MODE_NOT_ALLOWED`.

The same mode and cancel reason are on the trading WebSocket's SBE schema
as an additive change: it keeps schema version 36 and raises
`semanticVersion` to 1.2.0, with `STPMode` `DECREMENT` = 4 and
`CancelReason` `STP_DECREMENT` = 19.

## How clients use it

Set `selfTradePreventionMode` to `DECREMENT` on a create, over ConnectRPC:

```http
POST https://<api-host>/orders.v1.OrdersService/CreateOrder
Content-Type: application/json

{"order":{"symbolId":1,"side":"BUY","baseQtyScaled":"300000","limitGtc":{"priceTicks":"6100025"},"clientOrderId":"dec-1","feeAsset":"QUOTE","selfTradePreventionMode":"DECREMENT"}}
```

Or over REST:

```http
POST https://<api-host>/v1/orders
Content-Type: application/json

{"order":{"symbol":"BTC-USDT","side":"BUY","baseQty":"0.003","limitGtc":{"price":"61000.25","postOnly":false},"clientOrderId":"dec-1","feeAsset":"QUOTE","selfTradePreventionMode":"DECREMENT"}}
```

Batch creates take the mode per item, and a replace keeps the mode of the
order it replaces. Order reads, order history and private order events show
the mode as `DECREMENT`. A reduced order shows its lowered quantity: its
original quantity less what self-trade prevention removed. One canceled by
the cut ends `CANCELED` with `CANCEL_REASON_STP_DECREMENT`.

## Migration

No request changes: the mode and the cancel reason are new values. A client
that never sends `DECREMENT` can still see them on its resting orders,
because the incoming order's mode decides. When another subaccount of the
same root sends a `DECREMENT` order against a resting order of any mode,
that resting order's quantity is lowered, or it is canceled with
`CANCEL_REASON_STP_DECREMENT`. A client that maps `CancelReason` or
`SelfTradePreventionMode` exhaustively, or that treats a resting order's
quantity as fixed, should handle both.
