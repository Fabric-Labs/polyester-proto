## What and why

The private balance stream and `GetBalances` now keep three promises the
2026-11-19 balance-stream note already documented. No field, message or RPC
changes.

- **A trade credit is receipted only where the balance counts it.** A fill's
  credit (the base a buyer receives, or the quote a seller receives) can
  reach the account before the fill's settlement completes. Until then it is
  not yet in `trading` or `available`. A TRADING `BalanceUpdate`, or a
  `GetBalances` answer, used to carry the credit's leg and a
  `trading_applied_through_ts_ns` at or past it while its value still left
  the credit out. The next update, which had no legs, added it. Now the
  watermark stays just below that credit and the leg is left out until the
  update whose value counts it. That update carries the leg, with the
  account's balance after the transfer, and moves the watermark past it.
  So a leg or a watermark at or past a transfer's `ts_ns` always means the
  balance in that update or answer includes it.
- **Leg account.** Every leg on a TRADING update now says
  `"account": "trading"` on JSON and `account_code: TRADING` (301) on
  protobuf. Before, it said `"acct_code:1"` and `1`.
- **No empty FUNDING updates.** A FUNDING update is no longer published for
  an asset that has no Funding balance state yet (revision 0). Before, a
  Trading change published one such update per touched asset.

## How clients use it

Unchanged. Apply the receipt rule as documented: within the current
`trading_epoch`, a transfer counts as applied when its leg was received
(`transfer_id`, `is_debit`) or when a TRADING update or a `GetBalances` answer
carries a `trading_applied_through_ts_ns` at or past its `ts_ns`:

```
POST /ledger.read.v1.LedgerReadService/GetBalances
{}
```

A fill's debits (and its fees) can arrive in an earlier update than its
credit, and other updates for the account may come in between. When a
credit is held, its leg arrives with the update that applies the fill's
settlement. Until then, `trading_applied_through_ts_ns` stays below the
credit's `ts_ns` within the current `trading_epoch`; it never passes a
transfer that is not yet applied (see the field's comment on `BalanceUpdate`
and `GetBalances`). Each `complete` update is consistent with the legs
received so far; show Trading values only after one. A definitive settlement
refusal moves neither leg.

## Migration

None. A client that followed the documented rules already handles both
shapes. A client that decoded the leg account as the number 1, or showed
`"acct_code:1"`, should read `TRADING` / `"trading"` instead. A client that
counted FUNDING updates as activity sees fewer of them.
