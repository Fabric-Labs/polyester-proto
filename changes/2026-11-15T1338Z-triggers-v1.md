## What and why

Comment-only change: the field descriptions of `triggers.v1` and `orders.v1`
requests now state the limits the API already enforces. No field, type,
default, limit or behavior changed. Clients and SDKs that cannot read the
validation rules can now take every limit from the description, as the TWAP
`duration_ms` ("at least 1000") and `slice_interval_ms` ("at least 100 and at
most duration_ms") descriptions already did.

The descriptions now state:

- Ranges: `LadderTrigger.levels` takes 2 to 100 levels, and
  `price_max_ticks` must be greater than `price_min_ticks`. Basis-point
  slippage caps and trailing distances take 1 to 10000. A trailing stop's
  slippage cap takes 1 to 9999 on either side, and a market order or a
  market-slice TWAP that sells accepts at most 9999.
- Positive values: prices, trigger and activation prices, trailing distances
  in ticks, slippage caps in ticks and quantities, including
  `max_quote_debit_scaled`, `quote_proceeds_scaled`, `after_match_id` and
  `through_generation`. `ModifyTrigger` still takes 0 to clear an activation
  price or a slippage cap, as its descriptions already said.
- Required fields: order and trigger sides, the `order`, `trigger` and `oco`
  of create requests, child executions, both OCO legs, and the expiry of a
  `LimitGtd` order.
- Identifiers: `client_order_id` takes 1 to 36 ASCII characters (up to 36
  where it is optional) from letters, digits, period, underscore, colon,
  slash and hyphen. Every `request_id` takes 1 to 64 characters from the same
  set. `client_trigger_id` takes 1 to 36 bytes in UTF-8.
- Lists and keys: batch creates take 1 to 20 orders, batch cancels and
  batch replaces 1 to 50 items, each replace targeting a different order. The
  `symbol_id` filters of `GetOpenOrders` and `GetOrderHistory` take at most
  100 distinct IDs. A cancel or modify names exactly one of `order_id` and
  `client_order_id`, a modify or replace sets at least one patch field, and
  `page_token` is at most 512 characters.
- Field pairs: `GetUserTrades` requires `symbol_id` with `after_match_id`,
  and an `end_ts_ns` other than zero must not be earlier than `start_ts_ns`.

## How clients use it

Nothing changes on the wire. A ladder with 2 to 100 levels is accepted; 1 or
101 levels are rejected with `INVALID_ARGUMENT`, as before:

```http
POST /triggers.v1.TriggersService/CreateTrigger
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "trigger": {
    "symbolId": 1,
    "qtyScaled": "500000",
    "clientTriggerId": "ladder-btc-1",
    "ladder": {
      "side": "SELL",
      "priceMinTicks": "76000000000000",
      "priceMaxTicks": "80000000000000",
      "levels": 5
    }
  }
}
```

## Migration

None: the contract is unchanged; only its descriptions are more complete.
