## What and why

**Availability:** devnet only. Not available on testnet yet; the service isn't deployed or routed there.

The approval card for an order can now draw the order instead of only
describing it: a TWAP's slices on a timeline, a ladder's levels on a price
axis, a stop-loss, take-profit or trailing stop against the current price.

- New oneof `ApprovalRequested.preview` with one member,
  `OrderPreview order_preview = 5`: the order a `place_order` approval places,
  as typed values. Omitted for approvals without a typed preview, such as
  cancellations.
- New message `OrderPreview`: market, symbol, base and quote asset, `side`
  (`orders.v1.Side`), `quantity` or `quote_amount`, `estimated_value`,
  `reserved_amount`, `reference_price`, and a oneof `kind`: `market`, `limit`,
  `stop_loss`, `take_profit`, `trailing_stop`, `twap`, `ladder`.
- New messages `MarketOrderPreview`, `LimitOrderPreview`, `StopOrderPreview`,
  `TrailingStopPreview`, `TwapPreview`, `LadderPreview`, `LadderLevel`,
  `AttachedExits`, `AttachedExit`, `AttachedTrailingStop` and
  `ReferencePrice`; new enums `ReferencePriceSource` (`BEST_BID`, `BEST_ASK`,
  `LAST_PRICE`, `INDEX_PRICE`) and `TriggerDirection` (`AT_OR_ABOVE`,
  `AT_OR_BELOW`).

Every value is computed from the exact order the user approves, never written
by the model, and states the same order as `summary` and `parameters`;
approving approves exactly it.

## How clients use it

Watch a run as before. An approval for a ladder now also carries:

```text
POST /ai.v1.AssistantService/WatchRun
{"runId": "6349022007070785130", "afterSeq": "0"}

{"event": {"seq": "4", "approvalRequested": {
  "approvalId": "4611686018427388100",
  "summary": "Ladder: buy 0.001 BTC on BTC-USDT as 5 limit orders from 60000 to 64000 USDT ...",
  "expiresAt": "2026-11-09T14:30:00Z",
  "parameters": [{"label": "Type", "value": "ladder"}, ...],
  "orderPreview": {
    "symbolId": 1, "symbol": "BTC-USDT", "baseAsset": "BTC", "quoteAsset": "USDT",
    "side": "BUY", "quantity": "0.001", "estimatedValue": "62",
    "reservedAmount": "62.0558",
    "referencePrice": {"price": "76015.35", "source": "BEST_ASK", "observedAt": "2026-11-09T14:20:00Z"},
    "ladder": {"lowPrice": "60000", "highPrice": "64000", "postOnly": true, "levels": [
      {"price": "60000", "quantity": "0.0002"}, {"price": "61000", "quantity": "0.0002"},
      {"price": "62000", "quantity": "0.0002"}, {"price": "63000", "quantity": "0.0002"},
      {"price": "64000", "quantity": "0.0002"}]}
  }
}}}
```

- Prices and quantities are exact decimal strings: prices in the quote asset,
  quantities in the base asset. Basis points: 50 is 0.5%. An omitted
  `max_slippage_bps` means the market's default cap.
- A TWAP places slice i (from 0) at i × `slice_interval_ms` after the first;
  `slice_quantity` is the first slice, later slices may be slightly larger.
- Draw the order when the client knows the `kind`; otherwise show `summary`
  and `parameters`, which stay the full description. Keep showing `summary`
  for text-only surfaces and accessibility.

## Migration

None: the oneof, messages and enums are new, and `parameters` and `summary`
are unchanged. A client built on the previous contract ignores
`orderPreview`.
