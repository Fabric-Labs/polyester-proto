## What and why

The Strategies Community: a user publishes one version of one of their
strategies as the strategy's public listing, and everyone can browse the
listings and read each one's code without signing in. Sharing publishes the
version's code and settings only. Everything else a listing shows is either
its own text (title, description and types) or the platform's work: the
markets and flow come from the version, and the results come from a backtest
the platform runs at each publish. That backtest always uses the default
window, the default settings, 1,000 of the quote asset and the standard fee
schedule, so every listing's results compare alike. The author never picks
them.

New RPCs:

- `PublishStrategyVersion`: publish a valid version of one of the caller's
  strategies whose behaviour examples pass, with a title, a description and
  types. Publishing again shows the new version on the same listing and
  starts its platform backtest. Session only.
- `UnpublishStrategy`: hide one of the caller's listings. Copies other users
  already made stay theirs. The next publish shows it again. Session only.
- `ListCommunityStrategies`: the public listings, most recently published
  first, filtered by any of some types or markets. No credentials needed.
- `GetCommunityStrategy`: one public listing with its published version's
  code, manifest, settings form, money rules, flow and platform backtest
  report. No credentials needed.

New messages and enums: `StrategyTag` (the fixed list of strategy types),
`StrategyListing`, `CommunityBacktest`, `CommunityStrategySummary`,
`CommunityStrategy`, `ListingCapDetail`, and the RPCs' requests and
responses.

`Strategy.listing` (field 12) gives the author their strategy's listing: its
ID, whether it is public or unpublished, and the version it shows.

New error codes: `LISTING_NOT_FOUND`, `LISTING_CAP_REACHED` (an account has
at most 20 public listings; `ErrorDetail.listing_cap` gives the counts),
`USERNAME_REQUIRED` (listings show their author's username), and
`PUBLISH_LIMIT_REACHED` (at most 20 publishes per UTC day; `retry_after` says
when the day resets). `DeleteStrategy` now also answers `STRATEGY_IN_USE`
while the strategy's listing is public.

## How clients use it

Publish a version of a strategy, from the user's own session:

```http
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/PublishStrategyVersion
{"strategyId": "...", "versionId": "sha256:...", "title": "Weekly DCA",
 "description": "Buys BTC every Monday unless RSI is high.", "tags": ["DCA"],
 "idempotencyKey": "..."}
```

The response's `listing.listingId` is the listing's public ID.
`GetStrategy` and `ListStrategies` return it as `strategy.listing` too.

Browse and read, without credentials:

```http
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/ListCommunityStrategies
{"tags": ["DCA", "GRID"], "markets": ["BTC/USDT"], "limit": 20}

POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/GetCommunityStrategy
{"listingId": "..."}
```

- **Paging:** the list pages with `pageToken` and `nextPageToken`, default
  20 and at most 100 per page, most recently published first. A token is
  bound to the filters it was issued with.
- **Platform backtest:** `backtest.status` is QUEUED or RUNNING until it
  finishes. Once DONE, lists show `backtest.metrics` and
  `GetCommunityStrategy` the whole `backtest.report`. A bot that can't be
  backtested on candles is published with `NOT_BACKTESTABLE` and
  `notBacktestableReason`, and shows no results. A backtest that ended
  FAILED says why in `backtest.failure`; one whose runs couldn't finish is
  run once more by the platform before it fails.
- **Unpublished listings:** `GetCommunityStrategy` answers
  `LISTING_NOT_FOUND`, for its author too. The author reads its state on
  their strategy.

## Migration

None: every RPC, message, field and value is new. Existing calls answer as
before, except that `DeleteStrategy` refuses a strategy whose listing is
public.
