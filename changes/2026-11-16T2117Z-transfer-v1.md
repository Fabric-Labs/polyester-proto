## What and why

`BatchCreateInternalTransfers` now moves a batch's transfers together:
either every leg is credited to its destination, or no leg moves. Before
this change, a batch held at most 10 independent transfers that ran leg by
leg. A leg could be refused, for example for insufficient balance, after
earlier legs had already moved. A batch now holds up to 500 legs and is all
or nothing.

Contract changes in `transfer.v1`:

- **Request.** `legs` holds at most 500 legs, up from 10. The legs debit at
  most 10 distinct source accounts and at most 50 distinct source account
  and asset pairs. A source, destination and asset combination appears at
  most once.
- **`InternalTransferBatchStatus`.** `BATCH_SUBMITTED` (1) is removed.
  `BATCH_REFUSED` (2) stays and now always means that nothing moved.
  `BATCH_PROCESSING` (3) and `BATCH_ACCEPTED` (4) are new.
- **`InternalTransferBatchLegStatus`.** `LEG_ACCEPTED` (1), `LEG_PENDING`
  (2), `LEG_REFUSED` (3) and `LEG_NOT_SUBMITTED` (4) are removed.
  `LEG_PROCESSING` (5), `LEG_CREDITED` (6) and `LEG_NOT_MOVED` (7) are new.
- **`InternalTransferBatchLegResult`.** `request_id` (3) is removed, and
  `accepted_at_ts_ns` (6) is renamed `credited_at_ts_ns`. `flow_id` (5)
  stays.
  `transfer_id` is the leg's stable transfer identifier. It is set while
  the batch is processing and once it is accepted.
- **`ErrorCode`.** `ERROR_CODE_BATCH_TOO_MANY_SOURCES` (37) is new.

No status claims more than is known:

| Batch status | Meaning | Legs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BATCH_ACCEPTED` | Every leg is credited. | `LEG_CREDITED`, with `transfer_id` and `credited_at_ts_ns`. |
| `BATCH_PROCESSING` | The outcome is not known within the request. The batch completes on its own and ends accepted or refused. | `LEG_PROCESSING`, with `transfer_id`. |
| `BATCH_REFUSED` | Nothing moved, and nothing will under this idempotency key. | `LEG_NOT_MOVED`. `error_code` is set on the legs that caused the refusal. |

## How clients use it

ConnectRPC:

```http
POST https://<api-host>/transfer.v1.InternalTransferService/BatchCreateInternalTransfers
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "idempotencyKey": "rebalance-0001",
  "legs": [
    {"destinationSmartAccountAddress": "0x<destination>", "assetId": 7, "amountE18": {"lo": "500000000000000000"}},
    {"subaccountId": "<source subaccount id>", "destinationAccountId": "<destination account id>", "assetId": 8, "amountE18": {"lo": "1000000000000000000"}}
  ]
}
```

REST, with whole-unit decimal amounts:

```http
POST https://<api-host>/v1/trading/internal-transfers/batch
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "idempotencyKey": "rebalance-0001",
  "legs": [
    {"destinationSmartAccountAddress": "0x<destination>", "assetId": 7, "amount": "0.5"},
    {"subaccountId": "<source subaccount id>", "destinationAccountId": "<destination account id>", "assetId": 8, "amount": "1"}
  ]
}
```

An accepted batch answers, in REST form:

```json
{
  "requestId": "<batch id>",
  "status": "ACCEPTED",
  "legs": [
    {"legIndex": 0, "status": "CREDITED", "transferId": "<transfer id>", "creditedAtTsNs": "1763330000000000000", "assetId": 7, "assetCode": "BTC", "amount": "0.5", "destination": {"rootAccountPublicId": "<id>", "smartAccountAddress": "0x<destination>"}},
    {"legIndex": 1, "status": "CREDITED", "transferId": "<transfer id>", "creditedAtTsNs": "1763330000000000001", "assetId": 8, "assetCode": "ETH", "amount": "1", "destination": {"rootAccountPublicId": "<id>"}}
  ]
}
```

Limits and refusals:

- More than 500 legs, more than 50 source account and asset pairs, or a
  repeated source, destination and asset: `INVALID_REQUEST`
  (`invalid_argument`, HTTP 400). The message names the limit.
- More than 10 source accounts: `BATCH_TOO_MANY_SOURCES`
  (`invalid_argument`, HTTP 400).
- A leg that fails the checks of a single transfer, such as an unsupported
  asset or a destination that is not whitelisted: `BATCH_REFUSED`. That leg
  carries its `error_code` and the other legs carry none. No step-up
  verification is used.
- A source account that cannot cover its legs when the batch runs:
  `BATCH_REFUSED`, and that source's legs carry `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`.
- A batch that could not complete: `BATCH_REFUSED`. Every leg carries
  `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE`, and its message says to submit again under a new
  idempotency key.

Retries, MFA and rate limits:

- A retry with the same idempotency key and the same legs returns the
  batch's current state. Different legs under the key are refused with
  `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`.
- The MFA rule is unchanged. Recent MFA covers a root owner moving funds
  between its own accounts or to whitelisted destinations. Any other leg
  needs one fresh step-up for the whole batch. Without that step-up, the
  answer is `AUTH_STEP_UP_REQUIRED`: retry with the same idempotency key
  and a step-up token.
- A batch counts once against the internal-transfer rate limit, with a
  weight of `max(ceil(legs / 50), distinct source accounts)`. It no longer
  counts one unit per leg. A batch from one source account is charged to
  that account's budget, the one its single transfers use. A batch from
  several source accounts is charged to their root account's budget.
- A batch that weighs more than its account's rate-limit burst is refused
  with `RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (`resource_exhausted`, HTTP 429) and no
  `Retry-After`: waiting does not admit it. Send fewer legs or fewer
  source accounts per batch.

## Migration

An existing client that sends 10 legs or fewer:

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every leg passes its checks | `BATCH_SUBMITTED`. Each leg accepted, pending or refused on its own. | `BATCH_ACCEPTED`, with every leg `LEG_CREDITED`. Or `BATCH_REFUSED`, with every leg `LEG_NOT_MOVED`. Or `BATCH_PROCESSING`. |
| A leg fails its checks | `BATCH_REFUSED`. The failing leg is `LEG_REFUSED`, the others `LEG_NOT_SUBMITTED`. | `BATCH_REFUSED`. Every leg is `LEG_NOT_MOVED`, and the failing leg carries `error_code`. |
| A source is short when the batch runs | Earlier legs move and later legs are `LEG_REFUSED`. | `BATCH_REFUSED`. Nothing moves, and that source's legs carry `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`. |

Every replaced value, old to new:

- `BATCH_SUBMITTED` becomes `BATCH_ACCEPTED` once every leg is credited,
  and `BATCH_PROCESSING` until then.
- `BATCH_REFUSED` stays `BATCH_REFUSED`, which now always means nothing
  moved.
- `LEG_ACCEPTED` becomes `LEG_CREDITED`.
- `LEG_PENDING` becomes `LEG_PROCESSING`.
- `LEG_REFUSED` becomes `LEG_NOT_MOVED` with `error_code`.
- `LEG_NOT_SUBMITTED` becomes `LEG_NOT_MOVED` without `error_code`.
- In REST: batch `SUBMITTED` becomes `ACCEPTED` or `PROCESSING`, and leg
  `ACCEPTED`, `PENDING`, `REFUSED` and `NOT_SUBMITTED` become `CREDITED`,
  `PROCESSING`, `NOT_MOVED` and `NOT_MOVED`.

Steps:

1. Replace the removed enum values with the new ones above.
2. Read `credited_at_ts_ns` (REST `creditedAtTsNs`) instead of
   `accepted_at_ts_ns`. Stop reading the per-leg `request_id`. Identify a
   batch by its `request_id` and a leg by its
   `transfer_id`. A batch leg is not readable with `GetInternalTransfer`:
   read a batch's state by retrying it under its idempotency key.
3. On `BATCH_PROCESSING`, retry with the same idempotency key and the same
   legs until the batch is `BATCH_ACCEPTED` or `BATCH_REFUSED`.
4. Treat `BATCH_REFUSED` as final for its idempotency key. To try again,
   submit the batch under a new key.
5. Handle the new `BATCH_TOO_MANY_SOURCES` refusal, and the 500-leg and
   50-pair `INVALID_REQUEST` refusals. A `RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` refusal
   without `Retry-After` means the batch outweighs the account's burst:
   split it instead of retrying.
6. A client that split work into batches of 10 to stay within the old
   limits can send up to 500 legs at once.
