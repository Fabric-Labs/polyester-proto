## What and why

Iceberg orders: a good-till-cancelled or good-till-date limit order can now
show only part of its quantity on the order book. The new optional
`display_qty_scaled` on `LimitGtc` and `LimitGtd` sets that part. The book
shows at most the display; once that shown part is fully executed, the next
part is shown at the same price, behind the orders already resting there.
Market, immediate-or-cancel and fill-or-kill orders have no display field.

Three new `ErrorCode` values name a refused display:

- `ERROR_CODE_ICEBERG_NOT_ALLOWED` (309): a display on an order that cannot be
  an iceberg order, for example one with attached take-profit, stop-loss or
  trailing stop orders.
- `ERROR_CODE_ICEBERG_DISPLAY_TOO_SMALL` (310): a display below the pair's
  minimum order quantity, or one that splits the order into more than 100
  parts.
- `ERROR_CODE_ICEBERG_DISPLAY_INVALID` (311): a display at or above the
  order's quantity, or not a multiple of the pair's quantity step.

The owner's `Order` gains `display_qty_scaled`, the display the order was
placed with, and `hidden_qty_scaled`, the part of `leaves_qty_scaled` the
order book does not show: the book shows `leaves_qty_scaled` minus
`hidden_qty_scaled`. Both are zero for a plain order, and the hidden quantity
is zero once the order has ended. Public market data still shows only the
quantity on the book.

The trading WebSocket carries the same: its SBE schema `tradews` moves to
version 44 (semanticVersion 2.0.0), a breaking change for SBE clients.
`CreateOrder` and each `BatchCreateOrders` item gain `displayQtyScaled`,
`OrderEvent` gains `displayQtyScaled` and `hiddenQtyScaled`, and three
`RejectCode` values carry the numbers of the error codes above.

## How clients use it

Place an iceberg order of 1 BTC showing 0.1 BTC at a time. ConnectRPC, with
quantities scaled by the pair's `base_quantity_scale` from `GetSpotConfig`
(8 here):

```http
POST https://<api-host>/orders.v1.OrdersService/CreateOrder
Content-Type: application/json

{"order":{"symbolId":1,"side":"BUY","baseQtyScaled":"100000000",
 "limitGtc":{"priceTicks":"61000000000000","displayQtyScaled":"10000000"}}}
```

REST takes the display as a decimal in base asset units, `displayQty`, on
`limitGtc` or `limitGtd`, in single creates and in each batch item:

```http
POST https://<api-host>/v1/orders
Content-Type: application/json

{"order":{"symbol":"BTC-USDT","side":"BUY","baseQty":"1",
 "limitGtc":{"price":"61000","displayQty":"0.1"}}}
```

The display must be below the order's quantity, a multiple of the pair's
quantity step, at least the pair's minimum quantity, and at least the
quantity divided by 100, rounded up. Omit it, or send zero, for a plain
order. A REST `displayQty` with more decimals than the pair's base scale is
refused `ICEBERG_DISPLAY_INVALID`. A negative display is refused
`VALIDATION_ERROR`. An order preview checks the display as the create would. The display cannot be modified; a replace keeps it.

Order reads, order history and the private order stream carry the new
fields; REST names them `displayQty` and `hiddenQty`, decimals in base asset
units, for example `"displayQty":"0.1","hiddenQty":"0.8"` on an order with
`"leavesQty":"0.9"`.

## Migration

ConnectRPC and REST clients: none. The fields and error codes are new, and
orders without a display behave as before.

Trading WebSocket (SBE) clients must move to `tradews` version 44 before the
release that ships it: readers check the schema version exactly, so codecs
of any earlier version no longer interoperate.

1. Regenerate the SBE codecs from the published `tradews.xml` at version 44.
2. Encode the new `displayQtyScaled` on `CreateOrder` and on each
   `BatchCreateOrders` item: zero for a plain order, the display for an
   iceberg order.
3. Decode the new `displayQtyScaled` and `hiddenQtyScaled` on `OrderEvent`;
   both are zero for a plain order.
4. Map the new `RejectCode` values `ICEBERG_NOT_ALLOWED` (309),
   `ICEBERG_DISPLAY_TOO_SMALL` (310) and `ICEBERG_DISPLAY_INVALID` (311).
