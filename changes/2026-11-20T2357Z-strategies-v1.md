## What and why

The backtest contract is reshaped before backtests open, so a strategy page
can show how a bot would have done: its equity against buy-and-hold, its
falls, every trade with its profit, and a range of results across trading
costs rather than one number. `StartBacktest`, `GetBacktest`,
`ListBacktestTrades` and `ExplainStrategy` still answer `UNIMPLEMENTED`
until backtests open, so no client can depend on the removed fields yet.

Only bots that trade with market orders, including exits on watched price
levels, are backtested. A bot's first limit order ends its backtest as
`NOT_BACKTESTABLE`.

- **New: `ListBacktestTrades`** lists every trade of a finished backtest, a
  page at a time. It replaces `BacktestReport.report_url`.
- **`BacktestReport` changes:**
  - **Removed:** `report_url` (12), and the string `evidence_grade` (2).
  - **Moved:** `attempts` (8) moves to `Backtest.attempts` (9), counted when
    the backtest is read.
  - **New:**
    - `evidence_grade` (13), the enum `EvidenceGrade`: `NOT_ENOUGH`, `LOW`,
      `MEDIUM` or `HIGH`, with `EVIDENCE_GRADE_UNSPECIFIED` never on a
      finished backtest;
    - `held_back_baselines` (14);
    - `scenarios` (15): low, base and high costs, each a `BacktestScenario`;
    - `equity` (16): the equity curve with buy-and-hold's, at most 1,000
      `EquityPoint`s;
    - `held_back_start` (17);
    - `window_max_drawdown_percent` (18), `window_max_drawdown_start` (19)
      and `window_max_drawdown_end` (20);
    - `held_back_seen` (21).
  - **Changed meaning:** `trades` (9) is now the first 20 trades of the
    whole window, the order `ListBacktestTrades` pages in.
- **`BacktestSummary`:** `report_url` (6) is removed.
- **`BacktestMetrics` gains** `sharpe_ratio` (7, unset when undefined),
  `realized_pnl` (8, a signed decimal string), `max_drawdown_start` (9),
  `max_drawdown_end` (10), and the period's exact `start_equity` (11) and
  `end_equity` (12): read the bounds there, since the equity curve is
  sampled. The tuning period starts at the initial balances, any base asset
  valued at the window's first reference open; the held-back period starts at
  the split, marked at the last close before it; every period ends at its
  last close.
- **`BacktestTrade` gains** `realized_pnl` (8, a signed decimal string, empty
  on a buy), `trade_index` (9) and `after_gap` (10).
- **`DataGap` gains** `affected` (4): the bot was exposed across the gap, so
  the result is less certain.
- **New `ErrorCode`:** `BACKTEST_NOT_DONE` (31).
- **New `Backtest.Status`:** `NOT_BACKTESTABLE` (5), with
  `Backtest.not_backtestable_reason` (10). A backtest that finds, while it
  runs, that the bot can't be backtested (for example at its first limit
  order) ends with this status instead of `FAILED`. It is not an error, and
  running it again gives the same answer.
- **New enum `NotBacktestableReason`:** why a bot can't be backtested on
  candles. The reasons are `REACTS_TO_TRADES_OR_QUOTES`, `READS_SOCIAL_SIGNALS`,
  `MIXED_QUOTE_ASSETS`, `NO_REFERENCE_CANDLES` and `PLACES_LIMIT_ORDERS`.
  - It is carried by `ErrorDetail.not_backtestable_reason` (9), with
    `NOT_BACKTESTABLE`, and by `DeployProposalSummary.not_backtestable_reason`
    (11).
- **Deploying without a backtest:** every one of those reasons exempts
  `ProposeDeploy` from `BACKTEST_MISSING`, so a bot that can't be backtested,
  a grid bot included, can still be proposed and deployed. Its proposal has
  no `backtest`, and `not_backtestable_reason` says why. A page shows that
  reason to the user before they approve.
- **`BACKTEST_LIMIT_REACHED`** now covers both limits: 200 backtests started
  per UTC day, when `retry_after` says when the day resets; and 3 queued or
  running at once, when `retry_after` is unset. A request that returns an
  existing backtest never counts; starting a failed backtest again under a
  new key does.
- **New `ErrorDetail.backtest_limit` (10), a `BacktestLimitDetail`**
  (`daily_limit`, `daily_used`, `active_limit`, `active`), set with
  `BACKTEST_LIMIT_REACHED`, so a page can show "200 of 200 today" or "3 of 3
  running" without reading the docs.
- **New messages:** `BacktestLimitDetail`, `BacktestScenario`, `EquityPoint`,
  `ListBacktestTradesRequest` and `ListBacktestTradesResponse`.

## How clients use it

Start a backtest, read it until it is done, then page through its trades:

```http
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/StartBacktest
Content-Type: application/json
Connect-Protocol-Version: 1

{"versionId": "sha256:…", "idempotencyKey": "bt-1"}
```

```http
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/GetBacktest
Content-Type: application/json
Connect-Protocol-Version: 1

{"backtestId": "…"}
```

```json
{"backtest": {"backtestId": "…", "status": "DONE", "attempts": 3,
  "report": {"evidenceGrade": "MEDIUM",
    "scenarios": [{"name": "low", "slippageBps": 5, "metrics": {"returnPercent": "14.1"}},
                  {"name": "base", "slippageBps": 15, "metrics": {"returnPercent": "12.0"}},
                  {"name": "high", "slippageBps": 40, "metrics": {"returnPercent": "8.3"}}],
    "equity": [{"time": "…", "equity": "500", "buyAndHoldEquity": "500"}, …],
    "trades": [{"tradeIndex": "0", "market": "BTC/USDT", "side": "BUY", …}, …]}}}
```

```http
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/ListBacktestTrades
Content-Type: application/json
Connect-Protocol-Version: 1

{"backtestId": "…", "limit": 100}
```

```json
{"trades": [{"tradeIndex": "0", …}, {"tradeIndex": "1", …}], "nextPageToken": "…"}
```

- **Paging:** `limit` defaults to 100, at most 1,000. Pass `nextPageToken`
  back for the next page; it is empty on the last page, and valid only for
  the same caller and backtest.
- **Before the backtest is done,** `ListBacktestTrades` is
  `BACKTEST_NOT_DONE`.
- **Amounts** are exact decimals in the bot's quote asset.
- A page that shows a backtest draws `equity` with `held_back_start` marked,
  reads the headline as the range across `scenarios`, and labels the
  held-back block "already seen during tuning" when `held_back_seen` is true.

## Migration

No client can have used the removed fields, since these RPCs answer
`UNIMPLEMENTED`. When building against them:
- read every trade with `ListBacktestTrades` instead of `report_url`;
- read the grade from the `evidence_grade` enum;
- read `attempts` from `Backtest`.
