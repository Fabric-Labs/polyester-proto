## What and why

Connected apps can now call `StrategiesService` for the user: until now,
every call refused a connected app's session. The wire format is unchanged:
no message, field or RPC changed, only who may make each call. The
`StrategiesService` comments say which calls a connected app can make.

- `GetStrategyReference` and `ListStrategyTemplates` need no credentials.
- With the `account:read` scope, a connected app reads strategies and their
  revisions, strategy versions, deploy proposals and their status, bots and
  the versions a bot ran.
- With `strategies:manage`, which an app asks for only together with
  `account:read`, it also creates, saves and renames strategies, runs their
  versions' behaviour examples, proposes deploys and stops bots. Stopping a
  bot only ever reduces what it does.
- `StartBacktest`, `GetBacktest`, `ExplainStrategy` and `ListBotLogs` are
  classified the same way (`StartBacktest` under `strategies:manage`, the
  other three under `account:read`), but they are not implemented yet: a
  valid, authorized request to one of them gets `UNIMPLEMENTED` today.

A call outside the session's scopes refuses it with `PERMISSION_DENIED` and
`AUTH_SESSION_KIND_NOT_ALLOWED`. A connected app still can't deploy a bot or
move funds.

## How clients use it

A connected app granted `account:read strategies:manage` stops a bot with
its session token, as the user's own session does:

```text
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/StopBot
Authorization: Bearer <connected app session token>
{"botId": "<bot ID>", "mode": "KEEP_ORDERS", "idempotencyKey": "<key>"}
```

The same call with an `account:read`-only session answers
`PERMISSION_DENIED` with `AUTH_SESSION_KIND_NOT_ALLOWED`.

## Migration

None: no request or response changed.
