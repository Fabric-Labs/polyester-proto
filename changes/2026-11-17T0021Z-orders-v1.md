## What and why

This is a documentation-only change; no field, message or behaviour
changes. The documentation now states how a market order's protection price
relates to the pair's tick size:

- A market order's client reference price (`client_ref_price_ticks`, REST
  `clientRefPrice`) and its absolute maximum slippage (`max_slippage_ticks`,
  REST `maxSlippageTicks`) don't have to be multiples of the pair's tick size.
  The same holds for the absolute slippage of trigger, TWAP and trailing-stop
  market children. A limit price still has to be on the tick.
- The protection price is the reference moved by the slippage (up for a BUY,
  down for a SELL), then put on the pair's tick grid toward the reference: a
  BUY's is rounded down and a SELL's up. It never allows more than the
  slippage and can be up to one tick tighter. The order's `price_ticks`
  (REST `price`) shows it.
- The "ticks" in these field names are 1e-9 quote units, not the pair's tick
  size.
- Order reads echo the reference and the slippage exactly as given
  (`market_client_ref_price_ticks` and `market_max_slippage_ticks`, REST
  `marketClientRefPrice` and `marketMaxSlippageTicks`).

## How clients use it

On a pair whose tick size is 0.01, a market order with a reference of
73840.125 and an absolute slippage of 50.12 is accepted. The examples below
show the BUY.

```text
POST /orders.v1.OrdersService/CreateOrder
{"order": {"symbolId": 1, "side": "BUY", "baseQtyScaled": "8000",
  "marketIoc": {"clientRefPriceTicks": "73840125000000",
                "maxSlippageTicks": "50120000000"}}}

POST /v1/orders
{"order": {"symbol": "BTC-USDT", "side": "BUY", "baseQty": "0.00008",
  "marketIoc": {"clientRefPrice": "73840.125", "maxSlippageTicks": 50120000000}}}
```

Its protection price, and the order's `price`, is 73890.24 (73890.245
rounded down). A SELL with the same values gets 73790.01 (73790.005 rounded
up).

## Migration

None: behaviour is unchanged. A client that rounded its reference price or
slippage onto the tick before sending can keep doing so, or send the exact
values.
