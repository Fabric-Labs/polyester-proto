## What and why

A bot's list and page now show what the bot has been doing.

- **`Bot.activity`** is served: `ListBots` and `GetBot` return each bot's
  last 24 hours, oldest first, ending with the current UTC hour, an hour it
  did nothing in with zero counts. Counts lag the bot by up to about a
  minute.
- **`GetBotResponse.decisions`** and **`GetBotResponse.errors`** are served:
  the bot's last 20 of each, newest first, from the run it is on now. A bot
  deployed again starts a new list.
- **`BotDecision.orders`** has a documented shape, that of the manifest's
  behaviour examples, with what a live call adds. An order is
  `{"buy" or "sell": market, "qty" or "quote", "limit", "tif", "post_only",
  "max_slippage_bps", "stop_loss", "take_profit", "reason",
  "client_order_id"}`. A trigger is `{"buy" or "sell": market, "above" or
  "below", "qty", "limit", "tif", "price_source", "reason",
  "client_trigger_id"}`. A cancel is `{"cancel": id}`, and an order refused
  before it was sent is `{"refused": market, "client_order_id", "reason"}`.
  A key is left out when unset; amounts and prices are decimal strings. An
  order on a market the service doesn't know names it `"market #<id>"`,
  shows its amounts and prices as unscaled integers, and carries
  `"unscaled": true`.

## How clients use it

```http
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/GetBot
Content-Type: application/json

{"botId": "…"}
```

```json
{"bot": {"botId": "…", "activity": [
  {"start": "2026-11-21T02:00:00Z", "decisions": 0, "fills": 0, "expired": 0},
  {"start": "2026-11-22T01:00:00Z", "decisions": 60, "fills": 3, "expired": 0}
]},
 "decisions": [{"decidedAt": "2026-11-22T01:15:00Z", "event": "on_timer",
   "orders": [{"buy": "BTC/USDT", "qty": "0.125", "limit": "95000.5",
     "tif": "GTC", "reason": "dip", "client_order_id": "…"}],
   "logs": ["bought the dip"]}],
 "errors": []}
```

`ListBots` and `GetBot` are ConnectRPC only. There are always 24 entries in
`activity`.

## Migration

None: the fields were empty before.
