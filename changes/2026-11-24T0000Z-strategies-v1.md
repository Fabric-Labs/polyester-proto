## What and why

Forking a community strategy: a user copies a public listing's published
version into a new strategy of their own, and runs their own copy. Nobody
runs code they haven't seen. The copy keeps its attribution, and listings
show how many accounts forked them.

- `ForkStrategy` copies the version the listing shows into a new strategy of
  the caller as its revision 1. The copy is the caller's own: its behaviour
  examples and backtests are theirs to run. Forking one's own listing makes
  a plain copy and isn't counted as a fork.
- `Strategy.origin.fork` (`StrategyForkOrigin`) names the listing, its title
  and its author's username while the listing is public. Once it isn't, the
  strategy still shows it was forked, and which version it copied, but
  names the listing no more.
- `CommunityStrategySummary.forks` and `CommunityStrategy.forks` count the
  accounts that forked a listing. An account counts once, and the author
  never counts.
- `CommunityStrategy.forked_from` (`CommunityForkSource`) shows the listing a
  published fork came from. The platform sets it from the strategy's
  origin; the author can't change it. It names the source only while the
  source is public.
- `auth.v1.PolicyAction` gains `FORK_STRATEGIES`, the API-key permission for
  `ForkStrategy`.

## How clients use it

```http
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/ForkStrategy
{"listingId": "...", "name": "My weekly DCA", "idempotencyKey": "..."}
```

The response carries the new `strategy` and the `version` check of its
revision 1. Errors:

- `LISTING_NOT_FOUND` for an unpublished or unknown listing;
- `STRATEGY_CAP_REACHED` at 100 strategies;
- `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT` for a key reused with another request. The key
  space is shared with `CreateStrategy` and `SaveDraftVersion`.

The call is open to the user's session and to API keys scoped to the main
account whose policy grants `FORK_STRATEGIES`. Connected apps can't fork:
`AUTH_SESSION_KIND_NOT_ALLOWED`.

## Migration

None: the RPC, the fields and the action are new.
