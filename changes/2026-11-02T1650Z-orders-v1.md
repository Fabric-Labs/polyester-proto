## What and why

A BUY sized by a quote amount (`OrderIntent.max_quote_debit_scaled`, market
IOC or limit IOC) spends up to that amount at the prices it fills at. Until
now it ended `CANCELED` with `CANCEL_REASON_MARKET_REMAINDER` (market) or
`CANCEL_REASON_IOC_REMAINDER` (limit IOC) even when it had spent its whole
amount, with its filled quantity equal to its quantity and nothing remaining.
Clients that follow the documented meaning of those reasons showed a fully
filled buy as canceled for lack of liquidity.

Such a buy now ends `FILLED` once what is left of its amount buys no further
quantity step at the next price it could fill at. Its last fill reports no
remaining quantity, and no cancel follows it. Its quantity is what it bought.

A quote-amount buy that stops with part of its amount still able to buy more
keeps its partial fill and ends `CANCELED` as before:
`CANCEL_REASON_MARKET_REMAINDER` for a market order when the book runs out,
the next price is past its protection price, or it reaches the market's
maximum order quantity; `CANCEL_REASON_IOC_REMAINDER` for a limit IOC.

Buys sized by base quantity, sells and other order types are unchanged. No
field, enum value or message shape changes: only which existing status a
spent quote-amount buy ends with. The comments on `OrderStatus.FILLED`,
`CancelReason.CANCEL_REASON_IOC_REMAINDER`,
`CancelReason.CANCEL_REASON_MARKET_REMAINDER` and
`OrderIntent.max_quote_debit_scaled` now state this rule.

## How clients use it

Place the buy as before, then read its end with `GetOrder`. A buy of 100 USDT
that spent its amount reads `FILLED`, its filled quantity equal to its
quantity, nothing remaining and no cancel reason:

```bash
curl -sS https://<api-host>/orders.v1.OrdersReadService/GetOrder \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"orderId": "<order-id>", "includeExecutionHistory": false}'
# "order": { "status": "FILLED", "origQtyScaled": "128189",
#            "cumQtyScaled": "128189", "leavesQtyScaled": "0", ... }
```

The same buy on a book that runs out first reads `CANCELED` with
`"cancelReason": "CANCEL_REASON_MARKET_REMAINDER"` and its partial fill.

On the private order stream, a spent quote-amount buy's last event is its
`FILLED` event; it has no `CANCELED` event. Keep showing
`CANCEL_REASON_MARKET_REMAINDER` and `CANCEL_REASON_IOC_REMAINDER` as the
liquidity, price-limit or maximum-quantity stop they document.

## Migration

None: clients that handle `FILLED` for base-quantity orders handle these buys
the same way. A client that treated a canceled quote-amount buy with nothing
remaining as spent can drop that case.
