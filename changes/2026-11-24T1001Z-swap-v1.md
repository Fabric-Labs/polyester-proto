## What and why

A signed-in Swap's TWAP can now run for up to 24 hours, as a QuickSwap's
can: `TwapExecution.duration_ms` accepts 60000 (1 minute) to 86400000
(24 hours), up from 3600000 (60 minutes). Slices stay at least 10 seconds
apart. Auto-withdraw conversions keep their own 60-minute limit
(`conversion_twap_duration_ms`).

## How clients use it

Quote and create a TWAP of a day in five-minute slices:

```json
POST /swap.v1.SwapService/QuoteSwap
{"terms": {"sourceAssetId": 1, "destinationAssetId": 5,
  "amountE18": {"lo": "1000000000000000000"},
  "execution": {"twap": {"durationMs": "86400000", "sliceIntervalMs": "300000"}}},
 "maxSlippageBps": 100}
```

The slice count is `duration_ms` over `slice_interval_ms`, rounded up, and
every slice must be a valid order, so a long TWAP in short slices needs a
larger amount. A Swap lists at most 50 of its slices (`slices_truncated`).

## Migration

None: a wider accepted range; every request valid before stays valid.
