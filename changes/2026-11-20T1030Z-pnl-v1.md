## What and why

New `pnl.v1.ProfitAndLossService` reports the profit and loss of the main
account or one subaccount in USD:

- `GetProfitAndLoss`: PnL over a window (`TODAY`, `DAYS_7`, `DAYS_30`,
  `LIFETIME`), its realized part after fees and the change in unrealized PnL,
  fees and volume, by product and by market, and every asset held now with its
  quantity, quantity of unknown cost, cost, average entry price, current mark
  and unrealized PnL.
- `ListDailyProfitAndLoss`: the same per UTC day, oldest first, with the
  closing unrealized PnL of each day.
- `GetTradingStatistics`: trade count, volume, fees and realized PnL over a
  window.

Spot PnL uses the weighted average cost of each asset. Every trade is valued at
its execution time, every fee is a realized expense, and deposits,
withdrawals and transfers are never PnL: deposited coins have no known cost,
and a transfer between the main account and its subaccounts carries its cost.

The new policy action `READ_PNL` (`auth.v1.PolicyAction` 49) and subaccount
permission `SUBACCOUNT_PERMISSION_READ_PNL` (32) govern the reads. Like
`READ_BALANCES`, every subaccount role and every subaccount's read floor
include it, and the read-only assistant preset holds it. An API key needs it in
its policy.

## How clients use it

ConnectRPC, with a session token or a signed API-key request:

```http
POST /pnl.v1.ProfitAndLossService/GetProfitAndLoss
{"window": "PROFIT_AND_LOSS_WINDOW_DAYS_7"}

POST /pnl.v1.ProfitAndLossService/ListDailyProfitAndLoss
{"startTime": "2026-11-01T00:00:00Z", "endTime": "2026-12-01T00:00:00Z", "limit": 31}
```

REST: `GET /v1/pnl?window=DAYS_7`, `GET /v1/pnl/daily?startTime=…&endTime=…`
and `GET /v1/pnl/statistics?window=TODAY`, with REST enum values without their
prefix (`DAYS_7`, `SPOT`, `MARK_UNAVAILABLE`).

- `subaccount_id` is optional: omitted, a session or main-account key reads the
  main account with every subaccount it owns, and a subaccount key its own
  subaccount.
- USD amounts and quantities are base-10 decimal strings, signed where the
  comment says so. A figure that needs an unavailable price is omitted, never
  `0`, and `notices` says why.
- The daily list covers at most 366 days per request; `limit` defaults to 31.
  Its `page_token` is an opaque, exclusive keyset cursor bound to the caller,
  the subaccount selector and the requested days.
- `as_of` is when the figures were last published; figures follow settled
  trades within seconds, so a trade settled just before `as_of` may not be
  included yet.
- `LIFETIME` starts at 00:00 UTC of the day of the account's (or the selected
  subaccount's) first trade or transfer.

## Migration

None: the service and the action are new. Existing API keys keep their
policies; a key that should read PnL needs `READ_PNL` added.
