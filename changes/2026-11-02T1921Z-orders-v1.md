## What and why

Orders now carry their exact average execution price. `orders.v1.Order` has a
new field, `avg_price_e18` (`polyester.type.v1.U128`): the average execution
price across the order's lineage through this generation, in quote units
scaled by 1e18. It is the exact executed quote amount divided by
`cum_qty_scaled`, rounded half to even at the 18th decimal, and it is unset
while the order has no fills.

`avg_price_ticks` keeps its meaning: the same average in quote units scaled
by 1e9, rounded down. On pairs with a small price, such as one quoted to
nine decimals, rounding down to 1e-9 moved the shown average by several
basis points, and always in one direction. `avg_price_e18` does not.

The REST field `avgPx` keeps its name and type, a decimal string, and is now
rendered exactly from `avg_price_e18`: up to 18 decimal places, trailing
zeros trimmed, `0` with no fills. No field is removed.

Every order read carries both fields: the open-order, order-history and
single-order reads, and the order updates on the private WebSocket stream.

## How clients use it

ConnectRPC, JSON:

```bash
curl -sS https://<api-host>/orders.v1.OrdersReadService/GetOpenOrders \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>' -d '{}'
# {"orders": [{..., "cumQtyScaled": "7000000",
#   "avgPriceTicks": "2908",
#   "avgPriceE18": {"lo": "2908714285714"}, ...}]}
```

`avgPriceE18` is a `U128` of two `fixed64` words, `hi` and `lo`: the value is
`hi × 2^64 + lo`, and a zero word is omitted in JSON. Divide by 10^18 for the
price in quote units per base unit: here 0.000002908714285714. The same order
read over REST:

```bash
curl -sS 'https://<api-host>/v1/orders/open' -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>'
# {"orders": [{..., "avgPx": "0.000002908714285714", ...}]}
```

`GET /v1/orders/history` and `GET /v1/spot/orders/{order_id}` show `avgPx`
the same way. Private WebSocket order updates carry the same `orders.v1.Order`
with both fields.

## Migration

None required: the change is additive and `avg_price_ticks` is unchanged.
To show the exact average, read `avg_price_e18` instead of formatting
`avg_price_ticks`. REST clients that parse `avgPx` must accept up to 18
decimal places; a parser that assumed at most 9 must accept more, or round
to its own precision.
