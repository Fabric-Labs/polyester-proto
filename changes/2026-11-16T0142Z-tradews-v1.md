## What and why

On the trading WebSocket's protobuf frames, an order whose attached
trailing stop has a refused slippage cap or activation price now answers the
code a standalone trailing stop answers for the same mistake, as the Orders
API's REST and ConnectRPC endpoints already do. The trading engine judges
these fields for the WebSocket, so the change applies once the engine
answers them.

`create_order`, `modify_order`, `batch_create_orders` and
`batch_replace_orders` frames, for `attached_risk.trailing_stop` and
`new_attached_risk.trailing_stop`:

| Field | Refused when | Code | Before |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `max_slippage_ticks` | not positive | `SLIPPAGE_TICKS_INVALID` | `TRIGGER_PRICE_INVALID` |
| `max_slippage_bps` | not positive | `SLIPPAGE_BPS_INVALID` | `TRIGGER_PRICE_INVALID` |
| `activation_price_ticks` | negative | `TRIGGER_ACTIVATION_PRICE_INVALID` | `PRICE_TICK_SIZE` |

A cap of 10000 basis points or more still answers `MAX_SLIPPAGE_INVALID`,
a positive activation price off the pair's tick still answers
`PRICE_TICK_SIZE`, and an activation price of 0 still means none: the
trailing stop starts from the order's fill.

A standalone ladder whose `price_min_ticks` or `price_max_ticks` is off the
pair's tick answers `PRICE_TICK_SIZE` wherever it is refused, as the
Triggers API already answers it; a price that is not positive, or a maximum
not above the minimum, still answers `TRIGGER_PRICE_INVALID`.

The SBE frames carry no trailing stop or ladder, so nothing changes there.

## How clients use it

A protobuf `ClientFrame` whose order's attached trailing stop caps its exit
at 0 ticks:

```json
{"seq":"1","createOrder":{"order":{"symbolId":1,"side":"BUY","baseQtyScaled":"1000000","limitGtc":{"priceTicks":"70000000000000"},"attachedRisk":{"trailingStop":{"trailingDistanceBps":100,"maxSlippageTicks":"0"}}}}}
```

is answered with a `CommandReject` whose `code` is `SLIPPAGE_TICKS_INVALID`.
The order is not placed and nothing changes.

## Migration

1. A client that matched `TRIGGER_PRICE_INVALID` for an attached trailing
   stop's cap that is not positive must match `SLIPPAGE_TICKS_INVALID` or
   `SLIPPAGE_BPS_INVALID`.
2. A client that matched `PRICE_TICK_SIZE` for an attached trailing stop's
   negative activation price must match `TRIGGER_ACTIVATION_PRICE_INVALID`.

The frames, their fields and the WebSocket's status handling are unchanged.
