## What and why

Daily claim REST errors that carry no claims error detail, a request past
its deadline or a canceled one, now answer an uppercase code of the claims
error catalog instead of the lowercase transport name. The statuses and
details are unchanged; the `type` follows the code.

| Error | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A request that exceeded its deadline | `503` `deadline_exceeded`, `type` `.../deadline-exceeded` | `503` `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE`, `type` `.../service-unavailable` |
| A canceled request | `499` `canceled`, `type` `.../canceled` | `499` `INTERNAL_ERROR`, `type` `.../internal-error` |

## How clients use it

Branch on the uppercase `code`:

```text
GET /v1/trading/daily-claim

503 application/problem+json
{"type":"https://polyester.dev/problems/service-unavailable","title":"Service Unavailable",
 "status":503,"detail":"context deadline exceeded","code":"SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE"}
```

ConnectRPC is unchanged.

## Migration

A client that matched `deadline_exceeded` on `/v1/trading/daily-claim` or
`/v1/trading/daily-claim/claim` must match `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` instead and
retry as before.
