## What and why

While a service is starting or restarting, its REST routes now answer the
standard error body instead of an internal status document. This applies to
every Polyester API, the balances, balance history and transfers reads
included.

- **Before:** `503` `application/json` with an internal status document
  (`ok`, `service`, `uptime_ms`, `checked_at`): no `code`, `type` or
  Retry-After.
- **Now:** `503` `application/problem+json` with code `UNAVAILABLE` and
  `Retry-After: 5`.

## How clients use it

Retry after the Retry-After seconds:

```text
GET /v1/balances

503 application/problem+json
Retry-After: 5
{"type":"https://polyester.dev/problems/unavailable","title":"Service Unavailable",
 "status":503,"detail":"The service is starting or temporarily unavailable. Retry later.",
 "code":"UNAVAILABLE"}
```

ConnectRPC (`/ledger.read.v1.LedgerReadService/GetBalances` and every other
method) answers `unavailable`, as before.

## Migration

None: the status is unchanged and the earlier body was not documented. A
client that already handles a `503` problem with code `UNAVAILABLE` now
handles this one too.
