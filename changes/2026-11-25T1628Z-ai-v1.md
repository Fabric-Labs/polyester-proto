## What and why

A conversation without a subject now gets one when the assistant creates a
strategy in it: the conversation becomes about that strategy. A user who drafts
a strategy in a general chat and then opens it beside the assistant finds the
drafting conversation, with its whole history, instead of a new, empty one.

- The subject is set once, from none only: a conversation that already has a
  subject keeps it, and a second strategy created in the same conversation
  leaves the subject on the first. Another strategy is created in a new
  conversation.
- A strategy another conversation of the user is already about stays that
  conversation's: looking the strategy up returns that conversation, and the
  drafting conversation stays general.
- Saving a new version of an existing strategy changes no subject.
- A subject never changes once set, as before.

No message, field or value changes; only the documented behaviour of
`Subject`, `Conversation.subject`, `ListMessagesResponse.subject`,
`SendMessageRequest.subject` and `CONVERSATION_SUBJECT_MISMATCH`.

## How clients use it

Nothing new to call. Look the new strategy up by its subject,
`POST /ai.v1.AssistantService/ListConversations` with:

```json
{ "subject": { "strategy": { "strategyId": "1000" } } }
```

It returns the caller's one conversation about the strategy:

- When the strategy became the drafting conversation's subject (the drafting
  conversation had none, and no other conversation of the caller was about the
  strategy), that is the drafting conversation. Its `Conversation.subject` and
  `ListMessagesResponse.subject` then name the strategy, a `SendMessage` with
  that subject and no `conversationId` continues it, and messages that name it
  by its ID and omit the subject keep working.
- When another conversation of the caller was already about the strategy, it
  returns that conversation, and the drafting conversation stays without a
  subject.
- For a second strategy created in the same conversation, which keeps the
  first as its subject, it returns nothing until a message names the second
  strategy as its subject.

## Migration

None: no client must change. A client that keeps its own copy of a
conversation's subject should read it again from `ListConversations` or
`ListMessages` after a run that created a strategy (`SubagentFinished` with a
`draftVersion`), rather than assuming a general conversation stays general.
