## What and why

**Availability:** devnet only. Not available on testnet yet; the service isn't deployed or routed there.

`alerts.v1.PriceAlertService` manages durable price alerts: "tell me when
BTC-USDT falls to 90,000". An alert watches one market's last traded price or
index price, fires once when the price crosses its level, and is delivered to
the in-app inbox or the caller's linked Telegram account. Alerts survive
restarts and are evaluated by the exchange, not by the assistant.

The service has four methods:

- `CreateAlert`: create an alert. The current price is recorded as the
  alert's `reference_price_ticks`. An alert whose condition already holds at
  that price starts unarmed and fires only after the price moves to the other
  side and crosses back. Optional `idempotency_key` per caller.
- `ListAlerts`: the caller's alerts, newest first, keyset-paginated, with
  optional status and market filters.
- `UpdateAlert`: change direction, price or channels with a field mask and
  `expected_revision`. Updating a `TRIGGERED` alert sets it again.
- `CancelAlert`: cancel an active alert; cancelling a cancelled alert returns
  it unchanged.

Alerts belong to the caller's root account. A session or an API key of the
root account manages them; an API key scoped to a subaccount is refused with
`PERMISSION_DENIED` and `SUBACCOUNT_KEY_REFUSED`.

When `price_source` is not set, the alert watches the market's index price
where the market has one, and its last traded price otherwise; the returned
alert always states the price it watches.

The assistant's alert view now uses this service's message:
`ai.v1.View.price_alert` (`alerts.v1.PriceAlert`) replaces `View.alert_card`,
and `ai.v1.AlertCard`, `ai.v1.AlertDirection` and `ai.v1.AlertStatus` are
removed, so an alert has one definition.

Both directions are inclusive: `ABOVE` fires at price >= `price_ticks`,
`BELOW` at price <= `price_ticks`, as stop and take-profit triggers do. A
price that arrives late, or a feed that stops updating, never fires an alert.

## How clients use it

Session token or API key. Prices are quote units scaled by 1e9.

```http
POST /alerts.v1.PriceAlertService/CreateAlert
Content-Type: application/json

{"symbolId": 1, "direction": "BELOW", "priceTicks": "90000000000000",
 "channels": ["TELEGRAM"], "idempotencyKey": "run-7:tool-2"}
```

```http
POST /alerts.v1.PriceAlertService/ListAlerts
{"statuses": ["ACTIVE"], "limit": 50}
```

`ListAlerts` defaults to 50 and allows at most 200 per page; pass
`nextPageToken` back as `pageToken` with the same filters.

```http
POST /alerts.v1.PriceAlertService/UpdateAlert
{"alertId": "1234", "update": {"priceTicks": "91000000000000"},
 "updateMask": "priceTicks", "expectedRevision": "1"}
```

Errors carry `alerts.v1.ErrorDetail`: `ALERT_NOT_FOUND` (another account's
alert reads the same), `UNKNOWN_SYMBOL`, `PRICE_UNAVAILABLE` (retry),
`PRICE_SOURCE_UNSUPPORTED`, `ALERT_LIMIT_REACHED`, `REVISION_CONFLICT`,
`ALERT_CANCELLED`, `ALERT_TRIGGERED`, `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`,
`SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE`, `SUBACCOUNT_KEY_REFUSED`.

## Migration

`alerts.v1` is new. In `ai.v1`, which has not been published yet, read alert
views from `View.price_alert` (an `alerts.v1.PriceAlert`) instead of
`View.alert_card`; `AlertCard`, `AlertDirection` and `AlertStatus` are gone,
and `alerts.v1.AlertDirection` and `alerts.v1.AlertStatus` replace them.
