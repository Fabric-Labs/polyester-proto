## What and why

Users can protect a position they already hold with an OCO
(one-cancels-the-other): a take-profit and a stop-loss over one quantity,
created in one request. The quantity is reserved once, when the OCO is
created, and both legs share it, so it can be sold, or bought, only once.

- New RPCs on `TriggersService`: `CreateOco` and `CancelOco`, with the new
  messages `OcoIntent`, `OcoLeg`, `CreateOcoRequest`, `CreateOcoResponse`,
  `CancelOcoRequest` and `CancelOcoResponse`.
- `Trigger.order_list_id` (new optional field 9): the OCO a leg belongs to.
  Both legs carry it; neither has a parent order.
- `Trigger.client_list_id` (new field 66): the `client_list_id` the leg's
  OCO was created with. Both legs carry it; their `client_trigger_id` is
  empty.
- `ListTriggersRequest.order_list_id` (new optional field 6): lists both legs
  of one OCO.
- `ListTriggersRequest.client_list_id` (new optional field 7, 1 to 36
  bytes): lists both legs of the OCO created with that client list ID.
- Trade WebSocket (protobuf): `ClientFrame.create_oco` (19) and
  `ClientFrame.cancel_oco` (20) carry the same requests; `AckResponse.create_oco`
  (9) and `AckResponse.cancel_oco` (10) answer them.
- Trade WebSocket (SBE, `tradews.xml` schema 7, version unchanged,
  `semanticVersion` 2.2.0): new templates `CreateOco` (11), `CancelOco` (12)
  and `OcoAck` (31), the `TriggerPriceSource` enum, `CommandType` values
  `CREATE_OCO` (11) and `CANCEL_OCO` (12), and `RateLimitOperation` values
  `CREATE_TRIGGER` (9) and `CANCEL_TRIGGER` (10). Existing messages do not
  change.

Behavior:

- A SELL OCO protects a held base position: its take-profit is above the last
  price and its stop-loss below it. A BUY OCO spends held quote: its
  take-profit is below the last price and its stop-loss above it.
- Each leg places a market order (IOC) or a resting limit order (GTC) when its
  trigger price is reached. The exits pay their fees in the quote asset.
- The first leg to reach its trigger price places its order for the OCO's
  remaining quantity. **A partly filled order does not cancel the other leg:**
  the other leg keeps protecting the rest, and when its own trigger price is
  reached it cancels the working order and places its own for what is left.
  When the whole quantity has traded, the leg whose order traded last ends
  `COMPLETED` and the other ends `CANCELED` with reason `OCO`.
- Cancelling either leg with `CancelTrigger`, or the OCO with `CancelOco`,
  cancels both and releases the reserved quantity. `CancelOco` takes only an
  OCO's ID: a trigger ID, a leg's included, is refused `TRIGGER_NOT_FOUND`
  and cancels nothing.
- `CancelAllOrders` and the cancel-all-after timer cancel orders, not
  triggers: they leave an OCO with no working order alone. A working OCO order
  they cancel cancels the OCO.
- `ModifyTrigger` changes a leg's trigger price; quantity and side are not
  modifiable. A take-profit must stay on the profit side of the stop-loss,
  and of a price an unanswered modify of the stop-loss asks for (and the
  other way round), or the modify is refused `TRIGGER_PRICE_INVALID`.
- Each leg counts as one trigger against the trigger limits, so an account
  can hold at most 50 OCOs on one market, fewer beside its other triggers.

Not yet supported: a resting limit-maker take-profit leg (the take-profit is a
trigger with a limit exit), a trailing stop as the stop leg, and OTO or OTOCO
lists.

## How clients use it

Create a SELL OCO over 0.5 BTC held: take profit at 110000 with a limit exit
at 109500, stop out at 90000 at market.

```http
POST https://<api-host>/v1/triggers/oco
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "oco": {
    "symbol": "BTC-USDT",
    "side": "SELL",
    "qty": "0.5",
    "clientListId": "my-oco-1",
    "takeProfit": {"triggerPrice": "110000", "child": {"limitGtc": {"price": "109500"}}},
    "stopLoss": {"triggerPrice": "90000", "child": {"marketIoc": {}}}
  }
}
```

The response names the OCO and its legs:
`{"orderListId": "...", "clientListId": "my-oco-1", "takeProfitTriggerId": "...", "stopLossTriggerId": "...", "acceptedAt": "...", "acceptedAtTsNs": "..."}`.

ConnectRPC: `POST /triggers.v1.TriggersService/CreateOco` with
`{"oco": {"symbolId": 1, "side": "SELL", "qtyScaled": "50000000", "clientListId": "my-oco-1", "takeProfit": {"triggerPriceTicks": "110000000000000", "child": {"limitGtc": {"priceTicks": "109500000000000"}}}, "stopLoss": {"triggerPriceTicks": "90000000000000", "child": {"marketIoc": {}}}}}`.

- Prices are in quote units scaled by 1e9 on ConnectRPC and decimal strings on
  REST; the quantity is on the pair's quantity step.
- `client_list_id` (1 to 36 bytes) is the create's idempotency key: retrying
  the identical create returns the same OCO while either leg is active and
  for 60 seconds after both legs ended, whichever ended first, and a
  `ModifyTrigger` of a leg does not change that; a different create with the
  same ID is refused `CONFLICT_DUPLICATE_CLIENT_TRIGGER_ID`.
- A take-profit not on the profit side of the stop-loss is refused
  `TRIGGER_PRICE_INVALID`; free funds that cannot reserve the quantity are
  refused `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`; an exit that could never be placed is refused
  with its own code (`MIN_QTY`, `QTY_STEP_SIZE`, `MIN_NOTIONAL`). An OCO whose
  trigger price the last price has already reached is accepted and then
  rejected on both legs with `TRIGGER_WOULD_TRIGGER_IMMEDIATELY`: read the legs
  for their status. A create that was not confirmed in time fails with
  `TRIGGER_CREATE_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`: list the triggers by its client list ID
  (`GET /v1/triggers?clientListId=my-oco-1`, or `ListTriggers` with
  `client_list_id`) before retrying.

Read both legs: `GET https://<api-host>/v1/triggers?orderListId=<orderListId>`,
or by the client list ID: `GET https://<api-host>/v1/triggers?clientListId=my-oco-1`.
Each leg shows `"orderListId"` and `"clientListId": "my-oco-1"`.
Cancel the OCO: `DELETE https://<api-host>/v1/triggers/oco/<orderListId>`, or
ConnectRPC `POST /triggers.v1.TriggersService/CancelOco` with
`{"orderListId": "<id>"}`. A cancel of an OCO that has already ended answers
`TRIGGER_STATUS_INVALID`.

## Migration

None: the RPCs, messages, fields and WebSocket templates are new, and no
existing message changes. SBE WebSocket clients keep schema version 45; they
regenerate their codecs only to send the new commands.
