## What and why

A Swap can now be stopped. `CancelSwap` cancels one of the caller's Swaps
that has not ended, most usefully a TWAP: from the cancel on, no further
slice starts and no further first-leg order is placed. Nothing that
already traded is undone. A first-leg order placed just before the cancel
arrives may still be accepted, once; it is immediate-or-cancel and
finishes, and on a two-leg route a slice whose first
leg traded still sends its second leg for that slice's own intermediate
asset, converted where the Swap's price protection allows. Intermediate
asset carried from earlier slices is not converted after the cancel. The
untraded source amount, and any intermediate asset that was not converted,
stay on the account.

Added:

- `SwapService.CancelSwap` with `CancelSwapRequest` (`swap_id`) and
  `CancelSwapResponse` (`swap`).
- `SwapStatus.CANCELING` (not terminal: the cancel is taking effect) and
  `SwapStatus.CANCELED` (terminal). A canceled Swap shows `RECONCILING`
  instead of `CANCELING` while an order's outcome is being confirmed.
- `SwapReason.USER_CANCELED`, set with `CANCELED`.
- `SwapSlice.Reason.CANCELED`, for slots the cancel left unused and for a
  slice whose intermediate it left unconverted.
- `Swap.cancel_requested_at`, when the Swap was first canceled.
- `ErrorCode.SWAP_ALREADY_FINAL` (the Swap ended before any cancel; the
  error detail carries its `swap_id`) and `ErrorCode.SWAP_NOT_CANCELABLE`
  (an auto-withdraw conversion, which ends only with its auto-withdraw run).

The `SWAP` sub-account permission (`SUBACCOUNT_PERMISSION_SWAP`) now also
grants cancel. A sub-account's policy does not restrict a cancel, and a
cancel works on a sub-account that is not active: it only stops trading.

## How clients use it

Cancel a Swap by its ID, with a session token or an API key whose policy
grants `SWAP`:

```json
POST /swap.v1.SwapService/CancelSwap
{"swapId": "1234567890123456789"}
```

When the cancel is recorded, the response is the Swap with
`cancelRequestedAt` set: `CANCELING` while what was already placed
finishes, `RECONCILING` while an order's outcome is being confirmed (it can
move between the two), or already terminal. Follow
it with `GetSwap` until `isTerminal` is true. It ends `CANCELED` with reason
`USER_CANCELED`, its amounts showing exactly what traded; or, when the
cancel came too late to stop anything, with the outcome the Swap would have
had (`COMPLETED`, `PARTIALLY_COMPLETED` or `FAILED`); or `FAILED` with
`MINIMUM_NOT_MET`, which still applies to what traded.

A first cancel after the Swap's deadline records nothing and changes
nothing: a Swap that has not ended is returned as it is (`EXECUTING` or
`RECONCILING`) with `cancelRequestedAt` unset, and one that has ended answers
`SWAP_ALREADY_FINAL`.
A cancel usually takes effect within seconds; confirming an order's outcome
can take longer, and the Swap stays non-terminal until it is confirmed.

The cancel is idempotent: repeating a cancel that was recorded in time,
before or after the Swap ends, returns the Swap with its original
`cancelRequestedAt`, so an unknown outcome
(`UNAVAILABLE`, `DEADLINE_EXCEEDED`) is resolved by repeating the request.
Errors: `NOT_FOUND` `SWAP_NOT_FOUND`; `FAILED_PRECONDITION`
`SWAP_ALREADY_FINAL` or `SWAP_NOT_CANCELABLE`; `PERMISSION_DENIED`
`SWAP_NOT_PERMITTED`. A cancel is rate limited like an order cancel.

## Migration

Clients that read Swaps must handle the new statuses `CANCELING` (not
terminal) and `CANCELED` (terminal, with reason `USER_CANCELED`) and the
slice reason `CANCELED`, even if they never cancel: a Swap can be canceled
from another client. No existing field, value or call changes.
