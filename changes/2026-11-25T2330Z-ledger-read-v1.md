## What and why

Statement exports gain a third type, `BALANCES`: the account's balances over
the period, one row per account bucket and asset, so a client can show or
file the opening and closing position of a period beside its movements.

- `StatementExportType.BALANCES = 3`.
- Columns, in order: `account` (`funding` or `trading`), `asset`,
  `opening_balance` (at `startTime`), `credits` and `debits` (totals of the
  period's movements on the bucket, both as positive amounts),
  `closing_balance` (at `completeThrough`) and `movements` (their count).
  Every row reconciles: `closing_balance = opening_balance + credits −
  debits`, where the credits and debits are the LEDGER file's rows of that
  bucket and asset over the same period. Rows are sorted by `account`, then
  `asset`; a bucket and asset with a zero balance at both ends and no
  movement has no row.
- The file saves as `balances-<exportId>.csv`.
- A BALANCES period must start at or after the earliest instant whose
  balances the account history holds:
  - when the export is created, an earlier start is refused with
    `FAILED_PRECONDITION` and the new `ErrorCode`
    `STATEMENT_HISTORY_UNAVAILABLE` (REST `409`). The error detail carries
    that instant in the new `ErrorDetail.history_available_from`; REST sends
    it as the problem's `historyAvailableFrom` member;
  - if that instant moves later before the file is produced, the export
    ends FAILED with the new reason
    `STATEMENT_EXPORT_FAILURE_REASON_HISTORY_UNAVAILABLE`, and the new
    `StatementExport.history_available_from` says where to start a new one.

ACCOUNT_STATEMENT and LEDGER exports are unchanged.

## How clients use it

```http
POST /ledger.read.v1.StatementExportService/CreateStatementExport
Content-Type: application/json

{"type": "BALANCES", "startTime": "2026-11-01T00:00:00Z", "endTime": "2026-12-01T00:00:00Z", "idempotencyKey": "balances-2026-11"}
```

REST: `POST /v1/statements/exports` with the same body. A start before the
account history answers, in REST:

```json
{"type": "https://polyester.dev/problems/statement-history-unavailable", "title": "Conflict", "status": 409,
 "detail": "statement_history_unavailable: …", "code": "STATEMENT_HISTORY_UNAVAILABLE",
 "historyAvailableFrom": "2026-11-25T14:55:02.123456789Z"}
```

Offer the user "start at the earliest available", then create the export
again with `startTime` at or after `historyAvailableFrom`. Poll and
download as for the other types; every type has the same period limit (366
days), asset filter, limits on exports in progress, retention and
lifecycle.

## Migration

None: every change is additive. A client that never requests `BALANCES`
sees no difference.
