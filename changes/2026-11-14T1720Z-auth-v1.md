## What and why

Users can unlink or change their verified X/Twitter and Discord accounts.

- `UnlinkSocialAccount` now accepts `TWITTER` and `DISCORD` as well as
  `TELEGRAM`. Unlinking a linked X/Twitter or Discord account needs enrolled MFA
  and a fresh step-up proof in the `X-Auth-Step-Up` header.
- `StartSocialVerification` while an X/Twitter or Discord account is linked can
  change it, so it needs the same step-up. The linked account stays linked until
  the new verification succeeds.
- Two 30-day rules apply: an account unlinked from one Polyester account can't
  be linked to a different one for 30 days (the same account may reconnect it at
  once), and each account changes or unlinks each provider's account at most once
  every 30 days. A first link, or a link after an unlink, is not a change.
- New `AuthErrorCode` values `AUTH_SOCIAL_ACCOUNT_COOLDOWN` and
  `AUTH_SOCIAL_CHANGE_LIMIT_REACHED` (HTTP 412). `AuthErrorDetail.retry_after`
  says when the same request may succeed; REST answers carry a `Retry-After`
  header too. A verification refused for these reasons fails with that
  `error_code`, and `SocialVerification.retry_after` says when.

## How clients use it

```http
POST /auth.v1.SocialVerificationService/UnlinkSocialAccount
X-Auth-Step-Up: <fresh step-up proof>
{"provider": "TWITTER"}
```

REST: `POST /v1/auth/social/verification:unlink` with `{"provider": "TWITTER"}`.
To change an account, complete a fresh step-up, then call
`StartSocialVerification` with the step-up header and finish the verification as
before.

## Migration

1. Before unlinking or starting a verification while an X/Twitter or Discord
   account is linked, complete an MFA challenge for fresh step-up and send the
   proof in `X-Auth-Step-Up`. `AUTH_MFA_NOT_ENROLLED` means enroll MFA first.
2. Handle `AUTH_SOCIAL_ACCOUNT_COOLDOWN` and `AUTH_SOCIAL_CHANGE_LIMIT_REACHED`:
   show the wait until `retry_after`.
3. When a verification fails with either code, read `retry_after` on the
   verification.
