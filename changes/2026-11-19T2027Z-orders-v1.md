## What and why

Text only. `ERROR_CODE_CHILD_QTY_RAISE_NOT_ALLOWED` (orders.v1 `ErrorCode` 81)
now states the cap a raise is refused at. A ladder level's cap is its planned
quantity, even after the level was reduced. An exit order's cap is its current
quantity, because reducing an exit returns the reduced quantity to the legs
that protect the position. `TRIGGER_FAILURE_REASON_CHILD_QTY_RAISE_NOT_ALLOWED`
(triggers.v1 `TriggerFailureReason` 122) now says that a ladder's fills would
have gone past its total quantity, so the ladder ended.

The behavior now matches the planned-quantity wording: a ladder level that was
reduced can be raised back up to its planned quantity, and no further.

## How clients use it

Nothing new to call. Raise a ladder level with `ModifyOrder` as before, its
target total in `newQtyScaled`, scaled by the pair's `base_quantity_scale` from
`GetSpotConfig`:

```http
POST /orders.v1.OrdersService/ModifyOrder
{"orderId": "1234567890", "symbolId": 1, "requestId": "raise-1", "newQtyScaled": "10000000"}
```

With a base scale of 8, a level planned at 0.1 (`10000000`) and reduced to 0.075
is now accepted at 0.09 or 0.1, and refused with
`ERROR_CODE_CHILD_QTY_RAISE_NOT_ALLOWED` above 0.1.
