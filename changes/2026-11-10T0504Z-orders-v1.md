## What and why

DECREMENT self-trade prevention now supports attached protective legs and trigger children. Prevented quantity creates no trade or fee. A fully prevented attached exit keeps its position protected and may fire again when the surviving self maker stops leading the opposite book. A one-shot trigger remains completed once its child is admitted.

## How clients use it

Set the existing `selfTradePreventionMode` to `DECREMENT` on an order with attached risk or a trigger create. For example, include this fragment in the existing `/orders.v1.OrdersService/CreateOrder` request (`POST /v1/orders` over REST):

```json
{"selfTradePreventionMode":"DECREMENT"}
```

Live reductions retain the existing order update and prevention reason; zero remaining quantity is canceled with `STP_DECREMENT`, never reported as a fill. Attached exits retain their trigger and parent identity. Prevented exposure may remain reserved for a future protective exit.

## Migration

None: enum values and message fields are unchanged. Enable the mode for attached legs and trigger creates only once the environment deploys this behavior.
