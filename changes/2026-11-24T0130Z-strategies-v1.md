## What and why

Reporting community strategies, and moderation holds. A reader reports a
public listing to moderation, about the version they saw, with a reason from
a fixed list. Moderation can hold a listing: it leaves the community, and its
author sees why. No bot, copy or deployment changes.

- `ReportCommunityStrategy(listing_id, version_id, reason, note)` takes the
  user's own session only.
  - `reason` is one of `ReportReason`: MISLEADING_RESULTS, HARMFUL_CODE,
    SPAM, COPIED_WITHOUT_CREDIT or OTHER.
  - `note` is at most 500 characters, and required for OTHER.
  - One report per reader, listing and version, even after the version is
    published again: a repeat answers the first report's time and doesn't
    count toward the day's reports.
  - A reader makes at most 20 reports per UTC day.
- `StrategyListing.State` gains HELD, and `StrategyListing.hold_reason` says
  why. Only the author's own strategy shows it. A held listing leaves the
  list, and `GetCommunityStrategy` answers `LISTING_NOT_FOUND`.
- New error codes:
  - `LISTING_HELD`: publishing or unpublishing a held listing, or deleting
    its strategy;
  - `CANNOT_REPORT_OWN_LISTING`;
  - `REPORT_LIMIT_REACHED`, with `retry_after`.
- `DeleteStrategy` answers `LISTING_HELD` while the strategy's listing is
  held.

## How clients use it

```http
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/ReportCommunityStrategy
{"listingId": "...", "versionId": "sha256:...", "reason": "OTHER",
 "note": "The description promises a stop-loss the code doesn't have."}
```

The response's `reportedAt` is when the report was recorded. Take
`versionId` from the `GetCommunityStrategy` the reader looked at.

Errors:

- `LISTING_NOT_FOUND`: the listing isn't public.
- `VERSION_NOT_FOUND`: the listing never published that version.
- `CANNOT_REPORT_OWN_LISTING`.
- `REPORT_LIMIT_REACHED`.
- An API key or connected app is refused with
  `AUTH_SESSION_KIND_NOT_ALLOWED`.

An author's strategy can now show `listing.state = HELD` with
`listing.holdReason`. Publish, unpublish and `DeleteStrategy` answer
`LISTING_HELD` until moderation releases it.

## Migration

None: the RPC, the enum, the state, the field and the codes are new. A
client that switches on `StrategyListing.State` should treat HELD as
unlisted.
