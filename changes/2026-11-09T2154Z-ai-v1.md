## What and why

**Availability:** devnet only. Not available on testnet yet; the service isn't deployed or routed there.

The assistant can now swap one asset of the user's trading account for another,
within that account, only after the user approves the exact quote. Its approval
card can draw the swap instead of only describing it.

- `ApprovalRequested.preview` gains `SwapPreview swap = 10`: the asset spent
  (`source_asset`) and received (`destination_asset`), the `amount` spent, the
  estimated `expected_amount_out`, the binding `min_amount_out`, the accepted
  `max_slippage_bps`, the markets it trades on (`legs`: `SwapLegPreview` with
  symbol, side, average price, price impact in basis points, taker fee rate,
  fee and fee asset), and the quote's `quoted_at` and `expires_at`.
- New messages `SwapPreview` and `SwapLegPreview`.

Every value comes from the quote the user approves, never from the model, and
states the same swap as `summary` and `parameters`. Only `min_amount_out` binds:
what cannot trade at that rate stays on the account. The approval lasts as long
as the quote, 30 seconds after `quoted_at`; approving later swaps nothing.

## How clients use it

Watch a run as before. An approval to swap 100 USDT for BTC now also carries:

```text
POST /ai.v1.AssistantService/WatchRun
{"runId": "6349022007070785130", "afterSeq": "0"}

{"event": {"seq": "4", "approvalRequested": {
  "approvalId": "4611686018427388102",
  "summary": "Swap 100 USDT for BTC on trading account <the trading account's public id>: receive at least 0.00142 BTC (expected 0.00142714 BTC, at most 0.5% less), ...",
  "expiresAt": "2026-11-09T22:00:30Z",
  "parameters": [{"label": "Spend", "value": "100 USDT"}, ...],
  "swap": {
    "sourceAsset": "USDT", "destinationAsset": "BTC", "amount": "100",
    "expectedAmountOut": "0.001427142857142857",
    "minAmountOut": "0.001420007142857142", "maxSlippageBps": 50,
    "legs": [{"symbol": "BTC-USDT", "side": "BUY", "averagePrice": "70000",
      "priceImpactBps": 2, "takerFeeRatePercent": "0.1",
      "fee": "0.000001428571428571", "feeAsset": "BTC"}],
    "quotedAt": "2026-11-09T22:00:00Z", "expiresAt": "2026-11-09T22:00:30Z"
  }
}}}
```

- Amounts are exact decimal strings in their asset. Basis points: 50 is 0.5%.
- Show a countdown to `expires_at`: after it, the approval can no longer swap.
- Draw the swap when the client knows the preview member; otherwise show
  `summary` and `parameters`, which stay the full description.

## Migration

None: the member and messages are new. A client built on the previous contract
ignores them and shows `summary` and `parameters`.
