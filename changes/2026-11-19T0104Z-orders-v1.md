## What and why

Documentation only: no field, value, route or behavior changes. The
reference now states exactly when a modify amends an order in place and
when it replaces it, and what happens when the new total quantity is at or
below the order's filled quantity.

`behavior` selects the action:

| Behavior | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `AMEND_OR_REPLACE` (and unset) | Replace when the patch sets a price other than the order's, a total quantity above the order's total quantity, or a client order ID other than the order's; otherwise amend in place. |
| `AMEND_ONLY` | Amend when `AMEND_OR_REPLACE` would amend; a patch it would replace is rejected with `MODIFICATION_REQUIRES_REPLACE`. Other checks can reject the patch first with their own codes. |
| `REPLACE_ONLY` | Replace, even when the patch changes no value. |

An amend keeps the order under the same order ID with its queue priority; a
replace ends it and creates a successor under a new order ID. Either way, a
new total quantity at or below the order's filled quantity when the change
applies, fills after the request was sent included, ends the order at its
filled quantity (`CANCEL_REASON_QTY_AT_OR_BELOW_FILLED`) with no successor,
and `ModifyOrder` answers `AMENDED` with the order's ID. Every
`BatchReplaceOrders` item follows `AMEND_OR_REPLACE` and has the same
filled-quantity outcome. A batch result reports admission: an admitted item
can still be refused when it applies, leaving the order unchanged with no
order event.

Order the states of one order ID by `version`, keeping the highest applied,
as `Order.version` describes. A terminal status is final: no later state of
that order ID is open. Price and quantity order only two open states of one
GTC or GTD limit order at version 1 or above: its price stays the same and
its total quantity never rises, so the state with the larger total quantity
is the older. A modify's requested total orders nothing: placing 5, filling 2
and then amending the total to 1 ends the order at version 3 with a total
quantity of 2, above the requested total, and `ModifyOrder` answers
`AMENDED`, not an error.

Updated comments: `ModifyBehavior` and its values, and
`BatchReplaceOrderItem`.

## How clients use it

Over ConnectRPC only (the REST route takes its own request shape), lower an
order's total quantity in place with
`POST /orders.v1.OrdersService/ModifyOrder`:

```json
{
  "orderId": "1234567890",
  "symbolId": 1,
  "requestId": "amend-42",
  "newQtyScaled": "50000",
  "behavior": "AMEND_ONLY"
}
```

The response's `actionTaken` is `AMENDED` both when the order stays working
at the new total and when it ended at its filled quantity. The order's
events and reads tell the two apart: a `CANCELED` state with cancel reason
`CANCEL_REASON_QTY_AT_OR_BELOW_FILLED` (`QTY_AT_OR_BELOW_FILLED` on the
trade WebSocket) and a total quantity equal to the filled quantity
means it ended. Apply that state by its version like any other.

## Migration

None: the text describes existing behavior. A client that compares a state
with a modify's requested values, instead of with another open state or by
version, should stop: it can discard the order's current state, such as the
terminal state of an amend at or below the filled quantity, or the unchanged
order a refused batch item leaves.
