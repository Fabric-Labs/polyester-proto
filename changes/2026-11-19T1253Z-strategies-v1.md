## What and why

Users can now stop a bot. `StopBot` offers the two stops of a bot: keep its
orders in the book, protective stop-losses included, or cancel them all.
Either way the bot makes no new decisions and its holdings stay in its
subaccount.

- `StrategiesService.StopBot`, with `StopBotRequest` (`bot_id`, `mode`,
  `idempotency_key`) and `StopBotResponse` (`bot`, `mode`).
- `StopMode`: `KEEP_ORDERS`, `CANCEL_ORDERS`.
- `ErrorCode.BOT_NOT_RUNNING` (23): the bot has stopped, or isn't running
  yet. `ErrorCode.BOT_ALREADY_STOPPING` (24): the bot is already being
  stopped with the other mode.
- `PolicyAction.STOP_STRATEGIES` (50): the API-key permission to stop the
  main account's bots.
- `SubaccountPermission.SUBACCOUNT_PERMISSION_STOP_STRATEGIES` (33): the
  owner, admin, trader and leveraged trader roles now hold it, so a member
  with one of them on a bot's subaccount may stop that bot. Viewer and
  treasury don't.

Stopping only ever reduces what a bot does, so it takes no MFA.

## How clients use it

```json
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/StopBot
{"botId": "1234", "mode": "CANCEL_ORDERS", "idempotencyKey": "stop-7f3a"}

{
  "bot": {"botId": "1234", "name": "Weekly DCA", "subaccountId": "5678",
          "status": "STOPPING", "versionId": "…", "createdAt": "…"},
  "mode": "CANCEL_ORDERS"
}
```

- The stop is recorded before the call answers. The bot is `STOPPING` until
  every one of its orders has a final state and its fills are reconciled, then
  `STOPPED`; a stop has no deadline. Poll `GetBot` to follow it.
- `idempotency_key` is required, unique per caller's main account. The same
  key and request return the same stop, of the deployment the first call
  stopped, with its status as it stands now (`STOPPED` once it completed),
  even after the bot has been deployed again, and never stop it twice; the
  same key on another request is `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`. Don't compare a
  retry's response to the first one byte for byte: its status may have
  advanced.
- A second stop in the same mode with a new key returns the same stop
  without another one; one in the other mode is `BOT_ALREADY_STOPPING`.
- A bot the caller may not stop is `BOT_NOT_FOUND`, like one that doesn't
  exist.
- An API key needs `STOP_STRATEGIES` and must be scoped to the main account;
  the bot's own trading key can't stop it.

## Migration

None: the RPC, enum and enum values are new. Clients that list policy
actions or subaccount permissions see one more value of each, and an
existing role's permission list grows by `SUBACCOUNT_PERMISSION_STOP_STRATEGIES`.
