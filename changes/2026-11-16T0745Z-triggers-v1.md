## What and why

A ladder trigger now says how many of its levels were placed on the book,
rejected and canceled, and, once it has ended, how placement went. Before,
a ladder showed only its status, executed quantity and executed levels, so
a ladder with a rejected post-only level could only show as running, and
one that completed with some levels rejected looked like any other
completed ladder.

`LadderDetails` (a trigger's `ladder_state`) gains five fields and a new
enum:

- `requested_levels` (7): child orders the ladder requested, one per level.
- `placed_levels` (8): child orders placed on the book, open ones included.
- `rejected_levels` (9): child orders rejected before they were placed.
- `canceled_levels` (10): placed child orders canceled or expired, partly
  filled or not; when the ladder itself ends, its open child orders count
  here.
- `outcome` (11, `LadderOutcome`): `ALL_ADMITTED`, `MIXED_REJECTIONS` or
  `ALL_CHILDREN_REJECTED` once the ladder has ended;
  `LADDER_OUTCOME_UNSPECIFIED` while it runs.

A placed child order rejected after a partial fill counts as neither
rejected nor canceled.

## How clients use it

Read `ladder_state` on GetTrigger, ListTriggers and the realtime trigger
updates, as before.

```http
POST /triggers.v1.TriggersService/GetTrigger
{"triggerId": "..."}

{"trigger": {"status": "STATUS_COMPLETED", "ladderState": {
  "ladderLevels": 3, "executedLevels": 1,
  "requestedLevels": 3, "placedLevels": 2, "rejectedLevels": 1,
  "canceledLevels": 1, "outcome": "MIXED_REJECTIONS"}}}
```

REST (`GET /v1/triggers/{trigger_id}`, `GET /v1/triggers`) carries the same
fields under `ladderState`: `requestedLevels`, `placedLevels`,
`rejectedLevels`, `canceledLevels`, and, once the ladder has ended,
`outcome` with the values `ALL_ADMITTED`, `MIXED_REJECTIONS` and
`ALL_CHILDREN_REJECTED`; REST omits `outcome` while the ladder runs.

A completed ladder with `MIXED_REJECTIONS` reads as "Completed with
rejected levels (placed_levels/requested_levels placed)".

## Migration

None: the fields and the enum are new, and existing fields are unchanged.
