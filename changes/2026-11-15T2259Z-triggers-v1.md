## What and why

`ResumeTrigger` and `ModifyTrigger` accept an omitted `symbol_id` from an
API key limited to specific markets. Until now such a key was refused with
`ERROR_CODE_API_KEY_MARKET_DENY` unless it named the trigger's pair, even
for a trigger on one of its own markets, although `symbol_id` is optional.

The key's market list now applies to the trigger's own pair, whether or not
the request names it:

- A trigger on one of the key's markets resumes or modifies without
  `symbol_id`, as it does for a session or an all-markets key.
- A trigger on any other pair is refused with
  `ERROR_CODE_API_KEY_MARKET_DENY` (`PERMISSION_DENIED`), with or without
  `symbol_id`, and is left unchanged.
- A named pair outside the key's markets is still refused with
  `ERROR_CODE_API_KEY_MARKET_DENY`. A named pair that is not the trigger's
  is still refused with `ERROR_CODE_TRIGGER_SYMBOL_MISMATCH`.

Sessions and all-markets keys behave as before. The `symbol_id` field
descriptions on `ResumeTriggerRequest` and `ModifyTriggerRequest` now say
this.

## How clients use it

Resume or modify a trigger by its ID alone:

```http
POST /triggers.v1.TriggersService/ResumeTrigger
Content-Type: application/json

{"triggerId": "1234567890"}
```

```http
POST /triggers.v1.TriggersService/ModifyTrigger
Content-Type: application/json

{"triggerId": "1234567890", "triggerPriceTicks": "101000000000"}
```

The REST forms `POST /v1/triggers/{trigger_id}/resume` and
`PUT /v1/triggers/{trigger_id}` take no `symbol` either.

## Migration

None: a request that succeeded before still succeeds. Clients that added
`symbol_id` only to get past the refusal may drop it.
