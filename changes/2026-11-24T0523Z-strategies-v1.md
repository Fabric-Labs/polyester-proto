## What and why

Deploying a bot now moves the proposal's funding from the funding account
(the main account, or the proposal's `funding_subaccount_id`) into the bot's
new subaccount. The deploy goes on to start the bot only once the bot's
subaccount holds the funding. Until now the funding was moved separately.

- `DeployStatus.wait` (new, `DeployWait`): what a running deploy waits for
  from the user. `WHITELIST_REQUIRED` means the funding account's internal
  whitelist must list the bot's subaccount before funds can move into it,
  and `wait.address` is the address to add. The deploy stays at `FUNDING`
  meanwhile. Unset when the deploy waits for nothing.
- A deploy can now fail at `FUNDING`, with `failed_at = FUNDING` and a
  `failure` that says why: the funding account no longer holds the funding,
  the funding subaccount's policy doesn't allow internal transfers, or the
  bot's new subaccount couldn't receive its funding.
- A deploy can also wait at `FUNDING` with no `wait` while the bot's new
  subaccount becomes ready to receive its funding: nothing for the user to
  do, and calling `DeployBot` again goes on.
- When a deploy fails after its funding moved, its `failure` ends with "Your
  funding remains in the bot's subaccount; it will be returnable once returns
  ship." The funds aren't lost: they stay in the bot's subaccount.
- When a deploy fails before the funding's transfer is confirmed, its
  `failure` ends with "Whether your funding moved isn't confirmed yet; this
  deploy keeps its place among your bots until it is." Until then it counts
  toward the account's bot cap.
- `ProposeDeploy` refuses a funding subaccount whose policy doesn't allow
  internal transfers with `FUNDING_INVALID` on `funding_subaccount_id`.
  Allow internal transfers in its policy, or fund the bot from the main
  account.

## How clients use it

Call `DeployBot` as before. While `GetDeployStatus` (or `DeployBot`'s own
answer) shows `wait.reason = WHITELIST_REQUIRED`, ask the user to add
`wait.address` to their internal whitelist, then call `DeployBot` again with
the same proposal: the deploy continues from the funding step.

```http
POST https://<api-host>/strategies.v1.StrategiesService/GetDeployStatus
Content-Type: application/json

{"proposalId": "10871963286358685618"}
```

```json
{
  "status": {
    "proposalId": "10871963286358685618",
    "state": "FUNDING",
    "step": "Waiting for you to add the bot's subaccount to your internal whitelist",
    "wait": {"reason": "WHITELIST_REQUIRED", "address": "0x21a759df040a9b843de73092e59ac10ac17eb555"}
  }
}
```

A deploy with nothing to wait for has no `wait`.

## Migration

None: `wait` is a new optional field, and existing clients that ignore it
keep working. A client that shows `step` already shows the whitelist step
in words. Clients that moved a bot's funding themselves after a deploy must
stop doing it: the deploy moves the proposal's funding.
