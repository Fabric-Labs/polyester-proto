## What and why

A separate asset information snapshot supplies team-maintained descriptions, official links and known launch dates and prices. Load it independently when opening the asset Info tab. Existing trading and deposit/withdraw configuration responses are unchanged.

## How clients use it

ConnectRPC accepts an optional numeric asset ID filter. IDs match `ledgerId` in spot configuration.

```http
POST /marketdata.v1.MarketDataService/ListAssetInfo
Content-Type: application/json

{"assetIds":[1,2]}
```

The cacheable endpoint through the public gateway is plain REST GET, using repeated query parameters:

```http
GET /v1/assets/info?assetIds=1&assetIds=2
```

Omit `assetIds` to fetch all saved entries. At most 1000 positive IDs are accepted. Duplicate and unknown IDs are ignored. The complete finite result is ordered by ascending `assetId`, with no pagination. No authentication is required; responses carry `Cache-Control: public, max-age=60`.

Each `assetInfo` entry contains `assetId`, `editorial` and `editorialUpdatedAt` (UTC RFC3339). Editorial fields are `description`, `websiteUrl`, `whitepaperUrl`, `explorerUrls`, `xUrl`, `telegramUrl`, `discordUrl`, `githubUrl`, `issueDate` and optional `issuePrice` (`amount` as an exact decimal string, `currency` always `USD`). A known zero issue price is distinct from an absent price. `issueDate` is a calendar date, `YYYY-MM-DD`.

Assets with no saved information are omitted. Clearing all saved fields retains an entry with empty fields. Empty strings and collections and absent `issuePrice` mean unknown; hide the corresponding rows. Render descriptions as plain text. Descriptions allow at most 10000 characters; links are absolute HTTPS URLs of at most 2048 bytes, without credentials or whitespace; there are at most 10 explorers, in editorial order. Issue price amounts are nonnegative decimal strings of at most 64 characters, without signs or exponents.

## Migration

None: this adds a separate read operation. Existing configuration reads remain unchanged.
