## What and why

**Availability:** devnet only. Not available on testnet yet; the service isn't deployed or routed there.

A user can state an order's prices relative to the market or to the order
itself, for example "buy 0.01 BTC 10% below the current price, take-profit 5%
above, stop-loss 2% below". The assistant resolves each one to an absolute
price before the approval card, and the card can show where every resolved
price comes from.

- `OrderPreview` gains `repeated RelativePrice relative_prices = 18`: each
  price the user gave relative to another, in the order the prices appear in
  the order.
- New message `RelativePrice`:
  - `field` (`OrderPriceField`): which price of the order it is;
  - `price`: the resolved price the order carries;
  - `direction` (`PriceOffsetDirection`, `ABOVE` or `BELOW`) and
    `offset_bps`;
  - the `reference` oneof: `market`, a `ReferencePrice` with its source and
    `observed_at`, or `limit_price`, the order's own limit price.
- New enums `OrderPriceField` (`LIMIT_PRICE`, `TRIGGER_PRICE`,
  `ACTIVATION_PRICE`, `LADDER_LOW_PRICE`, `LADDER_HIGH_PRICE`, and the
  attached `TAKE_PROFIT_*`, `STOP_LOSS_*` and `TRAILING_STOP_*` prices) and
  `PriceOffsetDirection`.

The resolved price is the reference moved by the offset, rounded onto the
market's tick size in the direction never worse for the order that trades at
it: down for a buy, up for a sell. An attached exit trades the other way from
its order. The order is placed at exactly the resolved price the user
approved, even when the market moves afterwards.

## How clients use it

Watch a run as before. The approval for the example above carries:

```text
POST /ai.v1.AssistantService/WatchRun
{"runId": "6349022007070785130", "afterSeq": "0"}

{"event": {"seq": "5", "approvalRequested": {
  "summary": "Buy 0.01 BTC on BTC-USDT at limit price 67496.66 USDT, ... Limit price 67496.66 USDT is 10% below the best ask 74996.29 USDT at 04:13:50 UTC, rounded down to the 0.01 tick. ...",
  "orderPreview": {
    "symbol": "BTC-USDT", "side": "BUY", "quantity": "0.01",
    "limit": {"price": "67496.66", "timeInForce": "GTC",
      "attached": {"takeProfit": {"triggerPrice": "70871.5"},
                   "stopLoss": {"triggerPrice": "66146.73"}}},
    "relativePrices": [
      {"field": "LIMIT_PRICE", "price": "67496.66", "direction": "BELOW",
       "offsetBps": 1000,
       "market": {"price": "74996.29", "source": "BEST_ASK",
                  "observedAt": "2026-11-10T04:13:50Z"}},
      {"field": "TAKE_PROFIT_TRIGGER_PRICE", "price": "70871.5",
       "direction": "ABOVE", "offsetBps": 500, "limitPrice": "67496.66"},
      {"field": "STOP_LOSS_TRIGGER_PRICE", "price": "66146.73",
       "direction": "BELOW", "offsetBps": 200, "limitPrice": "67496.66"}
    ]
  }
}}}
```

- Prices are exact decimal strings in the quote asset. Basis points: 1000 is
  10%; an offset is 0 to 10000 above the reference and 0 to 9999 below it.
  Zero (omitted in JSON) is the reference itself, rounded onto the tick; a
  positive offset always resolves strictly on its side of the reference.
- `observed_at` of a `LAST_PRICE` reference (here and in
  `OrderPreview.reference_price`) is the time of the market's last trade, not
  when the assistant read it; show it, since a last price can be old.
- Show each resolved price next to the value it belongs to, for example
  "67,496.66 (10% below best ask 74,996.29 at 04:13:50)".
- `relative_prices` is empty when every price was given as an absolute price.

## Migration

None: the field, message and enums are new. A client built on the previous
contract ignores them, and the approval's `summary` and `parameters` still
state every resolved price with its reference.
