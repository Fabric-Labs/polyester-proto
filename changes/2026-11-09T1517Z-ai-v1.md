## What and why

**Availability:** devnet only. Not available on testnet yet; the service isn't deployed or routed there.

The assistant can now cancel every open order (or every one on some markets
or one side), cancel several orders at once, place several limit orders at
once, change the price or quantity of open limit orders, and modify, pause or resume a stop-loss, take-profit or
trailing stop, each only after the user approves exactly that change. Their
approval cards can now draw the change instead of only describing it.

- `ApprovalRequested.preview` gains four members:
  - `CancelOrdersPreview cancel_orders = 6`: the exact open orders the
    approval cancels (`OrderToCancel`: order ID, symbol, side, price,
    quantity, filled quantity). For a cancel-all, `cancel_all` is true and
    `symbols`, `side` and `live_triggers_kept` describe what it matched and
    what stays active (a cancel-all cancels orders only).
  - `OrderChangePreview order_change = 7`: each open limit order before and
    after (`OrderChange`: `price` → `new_price`, `quantity` → `new_quantity`,
    and `replaced` when the change gives the order a new order ID and resets
    its queue position).
  - `TriggerChangePreview trigger_change = 8`: one trigger's `before` and
    `after` `TriggerSettings` (status, trigger price, trailing distance,
    activation price, slippage cap in basis points or as a price distance,
    limit price), the `action` (`MODIFY`,
    `PAUSE`, `RESUME`) and the `reference_price` it watches.
  - `PlaceOrdersPreview place_orders = 9`: the limit orders the approval
    places together, each an `OrderPreview`. The exchange may refuse some
    and accept the others.
- New enum `TriggerChangeAction` (`MODIFY`, `PAUSE`, `RESUME`).

Every value comes from the exact change the user approves, never from the
model, and states the same change as `summary` and `parameters`; approving
approves exactly it.

## How clients use it

Watch a run as before. An approval to move a stop-loss now also carries:

```text
POST /ai.v1.AssistantService/WatchRun
{"runId": "6349022007070785130", "afterSeq": "0"}

{"event": {"seq": "4", "approvalRequested": {
  "approvalId": "4611686018427388101",
  "summary": "Modify stop loss Q9CpbPcdRV (sell 0.001 BTC on BTC-USDT): trigger price 60000 USDT → trigger price 62000 USDT, on trading account <the trading account's public id>.",
  "expiresAt": "2026-11-09T15:30:00Z",
  "parameters": [{"label": "Before", "value": "trigger price 60000 USDT"}, ...],
  "triggerChange": {
    "triggerId": "357578223783137780", "symbolId": 1, "symbol": "BTC-USDT",
    "baseAsset": "BTC", "quoteAsset": "USDT", "type": "STOP_LOSS", "side": "SELL",
    "quantity": "0.001", "action": "MODIFY",
    "before": {"status": "STATUS_ARMED", "triggerPrice": "60000"},
    "after": {"status": "STATUS_ARMED", "triggerPrice": "62000"},
    "referencePrice": {"price": "76015.35", "source": "LAST_PRICE", "observedAt": "2026-11-09T15:20:00Z"}
  }
}}}
```

- Prices and quantities are exact decimal strings: prices in the quote asset,
  quantities in the base asset. Basis points: 50 is 0.5%.
- Draw the change when the client knows the preview member; otherwise show
  `summary` and `parameters`, which stay the full description.

## Migration

None: the members, messages and enum are new. A client built on the previous
contract ignores them and shows `summary` and `parameters`.
