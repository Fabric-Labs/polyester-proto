## What and why

A signed API-key request is remembered until its receive window ends, so
that it cannot be replayed. That room is shared, and one key sending long
windows at a high rate could fill it, so other keys' signed requests were
refused as `SIGNATURE_INVALID`. Now the room is sized for the highest rate
tier, and each API key, and each account, holds at most a fixed share of it.
A key or account at its share has its own new requests refused, and every
other key is unaffected.

- New refusal `REPLAY_CAPACITY_EXHAUSTED`: the REST problem code with HTTP
  429, and `AuthErrorCode.AUTH_REPLAY_CAPACITY_EXHAUSTED = 57` (ConnectRPC
  `resource_exhausted` with `AuthErrorDetail`). The request was not
  processed. It replaces the `SIGNATURE_INVALID` a full room used to answer.
  Every service that checks signed-request replays answers it alike:
  orders, triggers, auth, deposit addresses, withdrawals, auto-withdrawals,
  guard signer, swaps and internal transfers, and the trading and market-data
  WebSocket upgrades (HTTP 429).
- A key's share holds the VIP3 rate limit at its full quota, 2,500
  placements and 5,000 cancels a second, with every request at the maximum
  60-second window: about 8,800 signed requests a second per key with a
  60-second window and a timestamp 10 seconds ahead, about 10,300 a second
  with a current timestamp, and about 62,000 a second at the default
  10-second window. An account's keys together hold twice one key's share.
- Deposit, withdrawal, guard-signer, swap and internal-transfer ConnectRPC
  calls now get every other authentication refusal as `unauthenticated` with
  its `AuthErrorDetail`, as orders and ledger reads already did. They used to
  get an HTTP 401 problem body with no detail.

Known limits:

- A key whose rate-limit override exceeds about 7,500 signed requests a
  second while sending 60-second windows can reach its share before its rate
  limit. A shorter window makes room sooner.
- Two or more accounts that each send at their full share together, about
  37,000 signed requests a second with 60-second windows, can fill the room.
  Every key is then refused `REPLAY_CAPACITY_EXHAUSTED` until their requests'
  windows end. No replay is ever accepted.

## How clients use it

Nothing to send. On `REPLAY_CAPACITY_EXHAUSTED` (HTTP 429 or ConnectRPC
`resource_exhausted` with `AUTH_REPLAY_CAPACITY_EXHAUSTED`), retry shortly
with a fresh timestamp. A shorter `X-API-RECV-WINDOW` makes room sooner.

```http
POST https://<api-host>/v1/orders
X-API-KEY-ID: ak_...
X-API-TIMESTAMP: 1762573080123
X-API-RECV-WINDOW: 5000
X-API-SIGNATURE: ...

HTTP/1.1 429 Too Many Requests
Content-Type: application/problem+json

{"code":"REPLAY_CAPACITY_EXHAUSTED","status":429}
```

## Migration

None: a client below its share sees no change. A client that treated every
401 as a credentials problem should handle this 429 as a retryable rate
refusal. A ConnectRPC client of the deposit, withdrawal, guard-signer, swap or
internal-transfer services can now read the refusal's `AuthErrorDetail`.
