## What and why

A bot's list and page show what the bot has been doing: how busy it was hour
by hour, and its recent decisions and errors.

- **`Bot.activity`** (new, not served yet): what the bot did in each of the
  last 24 hours, oldest first, ending with the current UTC hour, which is
  still under way. Each entry is a **`BotActivityHour`** (new): its `start`,
  its `decisions` (events the bot was called for, whether the call
  succeeded, failed, or ended before the bot's code ran), its `fills`
  (whether or not its code handles them), and its `expired` events (events
  it skipped because they were already too old when it could run, for
  example while it was behind). An hour it did nothing in is there with zero
  counts. A bot deployed again within the day counts every one of its runs.
  Counts lag the bot by up to about a minute. **`ListBots` and `GetBot` leave
  it empty for now;** a later change fills it in both, with its own note.
- **`BotDecision.snapshot`** (removed). It was never filled. A decision's
  snapshot, what the bot saw, can be as large as a megabyte and isn't carried
  in bot reads.
- **`BotDecision.event`** and **`BotError.event`** now name the handler the
  event woke, for example `on_timer` or `on_fill`. `BotDecision.decided_at`
  and `BotError.time` are when the event reached the bot. **`BotError.message`**
  is at most 512 bytes of UTF-8: the bot's own error, the rule its result
  broke, or a sentence saying the call ran out of its time or memory or could
  not finish. A decision keeps at most 20 log lines, each at most 256 bytes
  of UTF-8 and 1 KiB in all. A shortened line or message ends with `…`, and a
  last log line of `…` stands for the lines left out.

## How clients use it

Once served, `ListBots` returns each bot's `activity`, for a list's
sparkline:

```http
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/ListBots
Content-Type: application/json

{"limit": 50}
```

```json
{"bots": [{"botId": "…", "activity": [
  {"start": "2026-11-21T00:00:00Z", "decisions": 60, "fills": 3, "expired": 0},
  {"start": "2026-11-21T01:00:00Z", "decisions": 0, "fills": 0, "expired": 0}
]}]}
```

`ListBots` and `GetBot` are ConnectRPC only. Once served, there are always
24 entries per bot; until then, none.

## Migration

- Stop reading `BotDecision.snapshot`; it was always empty.
- Read `BotDecision.event` and `BotError.event` as a handler name, not a
  sentence.
