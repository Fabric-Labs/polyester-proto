## What and why

Strategies now names a market only by the exchange's symbol, base and quote
joined by a hyphen, such as `BTC-USDT`. That is the same spelling the SDKs,
the trading and market-data APIs, and the pair catalog (`PairConfig.symbol`)
use. The pair written with a slash between its assets is no longer accepted
anywhere. Nothing translates it, and there are no aliases.

A strategy version whose manifest names a market another way is refused with
`MARKET_INVALID` at its `markets[i]` path. When the name is the symbol written
another way, the issue gives the form to write instead: the pair written
with a slash, or in lower case, gets `use BTC-USDT`. The manifest's JSON
Schema in `GetStrategyReference` now gives the market pattern as
`^[A-Z0-9]+-[A-Z0-9]+$`. `ListCommunityStrategiesRequest.markets` takes the
same pattern: a filter naming a market any other way is `INVALID_ARGUMENT`
instead of a page with no listings.

Every field that names a market now carries the symbol: `MoneyCard.markets`
and `MoneyCard.events` (for example `candles BTC-USDT 1h`), `Bot.markets`,
`Strategy.markets`, `BacktestTrade.market`, `DataGap.market`,
`ErrorDetail.markets`, and flow node labels such as `RSI(BTC-USDT, 1d, 14) > 75`.
The bot API reference, the templates and the skill pack use the symbol in
every market call: `bot.price("BTC-USDT")`, `bot.buy("BTC-USDT", ...)`,
`bot.rsi("BTC-USDT", "1d", 14)`. Every bot call that takes a market,
`bot.position`, `bot.open_orders(market)` and `bot.cancel_all(market)`
included, raises on a market the manifest doesn't list instead of answering
a zero position, no orders or no cancels. Requests that carry `symbol_id`
are unchanged.

## How clients use it

Write markets as symbols in the manifest you save:

```http
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/SaveDraftVersion
{"strategyId": "...", "manifest": {"name": "Weekly DCA", "markets": ["BTC-USDT"], ...}, "code": "..."}
```

Take the symbol from the pair catalog (`PairConfig.symbol` in
`marketdata.v1.MarketDataService/GetSpotConfig`) rather than building it from
the base and quote assets.

## Migration

Replace every market name written with a slash between its assets, in
manifests, bot code and client-side market pickers, with the pair's symbol,
`BASE-QUOTE`. Stored strategy versions
whose manifests name markets with a slash are removed rather than migrated,
because a version's ID covers its manifest.
