## What and why

A SELL can now be sized by a quote amount: "sell 100 USDT worth". The new
sizing choice `quote_proceeds_scaled` on `OrderIntent` is the quote proceeds
target, in the pair's quote quantity scale. The order sells whole quantity
steps until its proceeds before fees, the sum of each fill's price times
quantity, reach the target, and it never receives more. The fee is charged in
quote on those proceeds, so the quote credited is the proceeds less the fee.
It is valid only on a SELL market IOC or limit IOC order, with no attached
risk and no peg. It works like Binance `quoteOrderQty` on a market sell.

The order holds the base its target sells at its limit price, or a market
order's maximum slippage price. It is rejected `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` when that
base is not free, so it never runs out of base while it trades. A target that
sells less than the market's minimum quantity and notional at that price is
rejected `QUOTE_SIZE_TOO_SMALL`. Once what is left of the target sells no
further step at the next price the order could fill at, the order ends
`FILLED`. If the book or its price limit stops it first, the rest is
canceled with `IOC_REMAINDER` or `MARKET_REMAINDER`.

The create and batch create responses echo `submitted_quote_proceeds_scaled`
(field 7), and `Order` shows it (field 41). For a SELL sized this way,
`cum_notional` is the proceeds it received before fees. The target less
`cum_notional` is what it did not receive.

Renamed, values unchanged:

- `ERROR_CODE_MAX_QUOTE_DEBIT_TOO_SMALL` (68) is now
  `ERROR_CODE_QUOTE_SIZE_TOO_SMALL`, the refusal of either quote sizing.
- `TRIGGER_FAILURE_REASON_MAX_QUOTE_DEBIT_TOO_SMALL` (24) is now
  `TRIGGER_FAILURE_REASON_QUOTE_SIZE_TOO_SMALL`.

The trading WebSocket changes too:

- Its protobuf encoding uses the same `OrderIntent` and responses.
- Its SBE `OrderSizingKind` gains `QUOTE_PROCEEDS` (3).
- The quote amount field of `CreateOrder` and `BatchCreateOrders` items,
  `maxQuoteDebitScaled`, is renamed `quoteQtyScaled`. It carries a BUY's
  debit limit or a SELL's proceeds target, as `sizingKind` says.
- `CommandAck` and `BatchCreateAck` results rename
  `submittedMaxQuoteDebitScaled` to `submittedQuoteQtyScaled`.
- The reject code `MAX_QUOTE_DEBIT_TOO_SMALL` (68) is now
  `QUOTE_SIZE_TOO_SMALL`.

## How clients use it

Sell 100 USDT worth of BTC at market, over ConnectRPC (BTC-USDT, quote scale
6):

```http
POST /orders.v1.OrdersService/CreateOrder
Content-Type: application/json

{"order":{"symbolId":1,"side":"SELL","quoteProceedsScaled":"100000000","marketIoc":{"maxSlippageBps":50}}}
```

Over REST the target is a decimal in quote units:

```http
POST /v1/orders
Content-Type: application/json

{"order":{"symbol":"BTC-USDT","side":"SELL","quoteProceeds":"100","marketIoc":{"maxSlippageBps":50}}}
```

The response's `resolvedBaseQtyScaled` is the base the order holds, the most
it can sell. `submittedQuoteProceedsScaled` (REST `submittedQuoteProceeds`)
echoes the target. Read the result with `GetOrder`:

- `cumQtyScaled` is the base sold;
- `cumNotional` (REST `cumQuote`) is the proceeds before fees;
- `feeTotal` is the quote fee charged on them.

Refusals use `order.quote_proceeds_scaled` (REST `order.quoteProceeds`) as the
field path:

- `VALIDATION_ERROR` with rule `sell_only` for a BUY, `ioc_only` for a
  resting or FOK order, or `no_attached_risk` for one with attached risk;
- `BAD_QTY` for a target that is not positive;
- `QUOTE_SIZE_TOO_SMALL` and `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` as described above.

On the SBE WebSocket, send `CreateOrder` with `side=SELL`, `timeInForce=IOC`,
`sizingKind=QUOTE_PROCEEDS`, `quoteQtyScaled=<target>` and `baseQtyScaled`
null.

## Migration

The renames break client code that names the old identifiers. Wire values
stay the same.

1. Replace `ERROR_CODE_MAX_QUOTE_DEBIT_TOO_SMALL` with
   `ERROR_CODE_QUOTE_SIZE_TOO_SMALL`, and
   `TRIGGER_FAILURE_REASON_MAX_QUOTE_DEBIT_TOO_SMALL` with
   `TRIGGER_FAILURE_REASON_QUOTE_SIZE_TOO_SMALL`. ConnectRPC JSON shows the
   new full names; REST error codes and reasons change from
   `MAX_QUOTE_DEBIT_TOO_SMALL` to `QUOTE_SIZE_TOO_SMALL`.
2. SBE WebSocket clients regenerate their codecs from the new `tradews.xml`.
   Rename `maxQuoteDebitScaled` to `quoteQtyScaled` and
   `submittedMaxQuoteDebitScaled` to `submittedQuoteQtyScaled`. The field
   ids, offsets and the `MAX_QUOTE_DEBIT` sizing are unchanged.
3. Clients that read a SELL's submitted quote amount read
   `submittedQuoteProceedsScaled`. `submittedMaxQuoteDebitScaled` is only
   ever set on a BUY.

No other change is needed: BUY quote sizing (`max_quote_debit_scaled`) is
unchanged.
