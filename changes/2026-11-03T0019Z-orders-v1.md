## What and why

A BUY sized by a quote amount (`OrderIntent.max_quote_debit_scaled`, market
IOC or limit IOC) can now carry `attached_risk` (take-profit, stop-loss or
trailing stop). Until now `CreateOrder` and `BatchCreateOrders` refused that
combination with `ERROR_CODE_BAD_QTY` "Quantity is invalid.", whatever the
amount, although the contract allowed it. `PreviewOrder` already answered it,
checking the legs against the pair's minimums as below; the submit now
answers it the same way.

The legs protect what the buy actually buys. A quote-amount buy has no fixed
quantity: it spends its amount at the prices it fills at, so its legs arm as
its fills settle and follow every later fill, as they do for any parent. When
the order is created, each leg's exit is checked against the pair's minimums
at the quantity the whole amount buys at the order's limit price, or a market
order's maximum slippage price: the least it buys if it spends the amount. An
exit below the minimums there refuses the order with the minimum's own code
(`ERROR_CODE_MIN_NOTIONAL`, `ERROR_CODE_MIN_QTY` or `ERROR_CODE_QTY_STEP_SIZE`),
as it does for a parent sized by base quantity.

No field, enum value or message shape changes. The comments on
`OrderIntent.max_quote_debit_scaled` and `RiskPolicy` now state this.

## How clients use it

Send the quote amount with the legs in one `CreateOrder`. The example buys
BTC-USDT with up to 50 USDT, a 2% maximum slippage, and an OCO take-profit
and stop-loss whose exits are market orders.

ConnectRPC (`POST /orders.v1.OrdersService/CreateOrder`) takes scaled
integers: `maxQuoteDebitScaled` is the amount scaled by the pair's
`quote_quantity_scale` from `GetSpotConfig` (50 USDT at a quote scale of 6 is
`50000000`), and prices (`clientRefPriceTicks`, `triggerPriceTicks`) are
scaled by 1e9. `symbolId` is the pair's numeric ID from `GetSpotConfig`.

```bash
curl -sS https://<api-host>/orders.v1.OrdersService/CreateOrder \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"order": {"symbolId": 1, "side": "BUY",
        "maxQuoteDebitScaled": "50000000",
        "marketIoc": {"maxSlippageBps": 200,
                      "clientRefPriceTicks": "77000000000000"},
        "attachedRisk": {"oco": true,
          "takeProfit": {"triggerPriceTicks": "80000000000000",
                         "child": {"marketIoc": {}}},
          "stopLoss": {"triggerPriceTicks": "74000000000000",
                       "child": {"marketIoc": {}}}}}}'
```

REST (`POST /v1/orders`) takes the same order as human-readable decimal
strings: `maxQuoteDebit` in quote asset units, prices in quote per base, and
the pair by `symbol`.

```bash
curl -sS -X POST https://<api-host>/v1/orders \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>' \
  -d '{"order": {"symbol": "BTC-USDT", "side": "BUY", "maxQuoteDebit": "50",
        "marketIoc": {"maxSlippageBps": 200, "clientRefPrice": "77000"},
        "attachedRisk": {"oco": true,
          "takeProfit": {"triggerPrice": "80000", "child": {"marketIoc": {}}},
          "stopLoss": {"triggerPrice": "74000", "child": {"marketIoc": {}}}}}}'
```

Both return the order ID and the legs' trigger IDs (`takeProfitTriggerId`,
`stopLossTriggerId`). The legs' quantity shown before the buy fills is the
quantity the amount buys at the order's price limit; once the fills settle,
the legs show and protect the quantity bought.

## Migration

None. A client that blocked the combination or converted the amount to a base
quantity before submitting can drop that step.
