## What and why

`ledger.v1.TransferCode` gains `FUNDING_OPENING_BALANCE` (1064), shown on REST as the transfer reason `funding_opening_balance`. It is the opening balance of a funding account: the balance the account held on Polyester Chain when its transfer history starts, credited from a system account. When an environment's account history restarts, each funding account's balance appears as one such credit, so the transfer history, the balance stream and profit and loss account for the funds the account already held.

Before this change those credits were not listed. The account's balance included the funds, but no transfer explained them, so profit and loss treated later sales or withdrawals of them as quantity it did not know of.

## How clients use it

`ListTransfers` lists the credit like any other transfer, and its `transfer_code` filter accepts the new value.

ConnectRPC `POST /ledger.read.v1.LedgerReadService/ListTransfers`:

```json
{ "transferCode": "FUNDING_OPENING_BALANCE" }
```

REST `GET /v1/transfers?transferCode=funding_opening_balance`.

The row credits the funding account (`is_debit` false), and its source is a system account.

## Migration

None: the value is new. A client that switches on `TransferCode` should show `FUNDING_OPENING_BALANCE` as an incoming balance, like a deposit, and keep treating unknown values as unclassified.
