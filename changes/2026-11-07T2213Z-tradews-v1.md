## What and why

A trading WebSocket session can now opt in to cancel-on-disconnect when it
connects, on either encoding (protobuf or SBE). While the session is
connected, the venue keeps a cancel-all-after timer of the whole selected
trading account armed with the session's grace period. When the connection
drops, by a socket close, a missed liveness check or a server restart, the
account's open orders are cancelled once the grace period passes, unless a
session of the account opted in again by then, on any connection. Orders end
`CANCELED` with cancel reason `CANCEL_ON_DISCONNECT`
(`2026-11-08T2026Z-orders-v1.md`); triggers are not cancelled.

This timer is separate from the account's own cancel-all-after timer
(`CancelAllAfter` on the Orders API and on the WebSocket): each has its own
timeout and scope, either can fire, and neither arms, refreshes or disarms
the other. Several opted-in sessions of one account share the
cancel-on-disconnect timer, each with its own grace period: the account's
orders are cancelled only once none of them is within its own grace period,
so a short session ending never cancels the orders while a session with a
longer grace period is still connected or within its grace. The scope is the
whole account the API key is bound to: for a subaccount key, that subaccount
only, never its siblings or its root account; for a root account key, the root
account's own orders, not its subaccounts'. No message or field changes.

## How clients use it

Add two headers to the signed WebSocket upgrade request:

```text
X-Cancel-On-Disconnect: ACCOUNT
X-Cancel-On-Disconnect-Grace-Sec: 15
```

The grace period is a whole number of seconds from 10 to 120.
`X-Cancel-On-Disconnect: NONE`, or no header, opts out. On success, the
`101 Switching Protocols` response echoes both headers with the session's own
grace period; a client must treat a `101` without them as not protected. A
refused opt-in refuses the connection, so a session never trades
unprotected: `400` with `BAD_REQUEST` (malformed headers) or
`CANCEL_ALL_AFTER_TIMEOUT_INVALID` (grace period out of range), `429` with
`RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, `503` with `UNAVAILABLE`, or `403` with the API
key's refusal code, as the response body.

The grace period counts from when the venue sees the session end. Reconnect
and opt in again within it to keep the account's orders. If the venue
itself fails, the orders may be cancelled up to a quarter of the grace
period earlier. The timer is kept alive by the venue every quarter of the
grace period, which counts against the account's cancellation rate limit,
as a `CancelAllAfter` does.

## Migration

None: the opt-in is new and off by default, and sessions without the
headers behave as before.
