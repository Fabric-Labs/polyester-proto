## What and why

Documentation only: no field, value, route or behavior changes. The
reference now says in which direction a standalone stop-loss or take-profit
fires. Before, it only said that it "fires when the price crosses a
threshold", and the create request has no direction field.

The strategy and the side of the trigger's order set the direction:

| Strategy | Side | Fires when the watched price | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `stop_loss` | SELL | falls to or below the trigger price | limit the loss on a held position |
| `stop_loss` | BUY | rises to or above the trigger price | breakout buy |
| `take_profit` | SELL | rises to or above the trigger price | sell into a rise |
| `take_profit` | BUY | falls to or below the trigger price | buy a dip |

The comparison is inclusive: a price equal to the trigger price fires the
trigger. Reads report the direction as `stop.trigger_direction` (`ABOVE` or
`BELOW`).

A trigger price the watched price has already reached when the trigger takes
effect is not refused by the create. `CreateTrigger` returns a trigger ID, and
the trigger then ends `STATUS_FAILED` with failure reason
`TRIGGER_FAILURE_REASON_TRIGGER_WOULD_TRIGGER_IMMEDIATELY`. It never fires,
places no order, and its reserved funds are released.

Updated comments: `TriggerType.STOP_LOSS` and `TAKE_PROFIT`,
`ConditionalTrigger` and its `trigger_price_ticks` and `side`,
`TriggerIntent.stop_loss` and `take_profit`, `Trigger.stop_loss` and
`take_profit`, and `StopDetails.trigger_direction`.

## How clients use it

Choose the strategy and side from the table. A BUY stop-loss that buys once
the price rises to 70,000, over ConnectRPC
`POST /triggers.v1.TriggersService/CreateTrigger`:

```json
{
  "trigger": {
    "symbolId": 1,
    "qtyScaled": "100000",
    "clientTriggerId": "breakout-1",
    "stopLoss": {
      "triggerPriceTicks": "70000000000000",
      "side": "BUY",
      "child": { "marketIoc": {} }
    }
  }
}
```

The REST route `POST /v1/triggers` takes the same intent with its REST
names (`symbol`, `qty`, `stopLoss.triggerPrice`). After the create, read the
trigger with `GetTrigger` or `ListTriggers`: `STATUS_ARMED` means it waits for
the price; `STATUS_FAILED` with
`TRIGGER_FAILURE_REASON_TRIGGER_WOULD_TRIGGER_IMMEDIATELY` means the price had
already reached the trigger price.
