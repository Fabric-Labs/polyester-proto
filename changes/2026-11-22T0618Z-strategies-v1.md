## What and why

A strategy now keeps notes: a one-line summary of what it is, and a Markdown
body with what it is meant to do, what was decided and what was tried and
dropped. The user writes them, and so do assistants acting for the user, so
the next edit of the strategy, from any conversation or tool, starts from
what was already decided.

- **`StrategyNotes`** (new): `strategy_id`, `notes_revision` (one per save
  that changed them, from 1; 0 before the first save; counted apart from the
  strategy's own revision), `summary` (one line, with no line break, of at
  most 120 characters), `body`
  (Markdown, at most 8192 bytes of UTF-8), `saved_at` and `saved_by`. Every
  revision is kept. A copied or forked strategy starts with no notes, and a
  community listing never shows them.
- **`GetStrategyNotes`**, **`SaveStrategyNotes`** and
  **`ListStrategyNotesRevisions`** (new) read, save and list them.
  `GetStrategyResponse.notes` (new) carries the current notes too.
- **`Strategy.markets`** (new): the markets of the current revision's
  version, as its money card names them, for example `BTC/USDT`; empty for a
  strategy with no revision or whose current revision did not validate.
  **`Strategy.summary`** (new): the notes' summary. Both are set only for a
  caller who may read the strategy, the same rule as `Strategy.bot`: a
  response to a caller who may only draft strategies leaves them empty.
- **`ListBacktests`** (new): a strategy's backtests, the backtests of the
  versions its revisions saved, newest first, each as a headline
  (`StrategyBacktest`: its `BacktestSummary`, version, the newest revision
  that saved that version, window and times). A backtest is listed for 30
  days after it finished; `GetBacktest` reads one whole.
- **`SaveDraftVersionRequest.expected_notes_revision`** and
  **`SaveStrategyNotesRequest.expected_revision`** (new, optional): a writer
  that measured a strategy's draft and notes together pins the other record's
  revision, so a save lands only while both are as it measured them.
- **`NOTES_REVISION_CONFLICT`** (new error code) with
  **`ErrorDetail.notes_conflict`** (`NotesConflictDetail`, new): the notes
  were saved since the revision the request was made from.

## How clients use it

Read a strategy's notes, then save onto their current revision:

```http
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/GetStrategyNotes
{"strategyId": "1234"}

POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/SaveStrategyNotes
{"strategyId": "1234", "expectedNotesRevision": "3",
 "summary": "BTC/USDT grid, 10 rungs 0.5% apart",
 "body": "## Intent\nKeep orders under 200 USDT.\n\n## Rejected\nA stop-loss: the user declined.",
 "idempotencyKey": "notes-7f3a"}
```

- `expectedNotesRevision` must be the current notes revision (0 before the
  first save). Any other value is `FAILED_PRECONDITION`
  `NOTES_REVISION_CONFLICT`, with the current revision in `notes_conflict`:
  read the notes again, apply the change, and save on the new revision.
- Notes equal to the current ones add no revision and return the current
  one. A summary with a line break of any kind (`\n`, `\r`, `\v`, `\f`,
  U+0085, U+2028 or U+2029) or over 120 characters, or a body over 8192
  bytes, is `INVALID_ARGUMENT` with a `buf.validate.Violations` detail
  naming the field (`summary` or `body`) and its rule (`string.pattern`,
  `string.max_len` or `string.max_bytes`); the body may hold line breaks.
- `GetStrategy` returns the strategy, its summary, its head version and its
  notes from one snapshot: they always belong together.
- `idempotencyKey` is optional and shares its namespace with
  `CreateStrategy` and `SaveDraftVersion`: the same key and request return
  the revision the first call saved; another request with the same key is
  `ALREADY_EXISTS` `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`.
- To save a draft and notes as measured together, set
  `expectedNotesRevision` on `SaveDraftVersion` and `expectedRevision` on
  `SaveStrategyNotes`: a moved draft is `DRAFT_REVISION_CONFLICT`, moved notes
  `NOTES_REVISION_CONFLICT`. Leave them unset otherwise.
- `ListStrategyNotesRevisions` pages newest first, 10 by default and at most
  20. `ListBacktests` (`{"strategyId": "1234"}`) pages newest first, 10 by
  default and at most 50. Both take `pageToken` from the previous response.
- `ListStrategies` is unchanged: up to 200 a page, 50 by default, so an
  account's 100 strategies fit in one page with `limit: 100`.
- Reads need `READ_STRATEGIES` (API keys) or `account:read` (connected
  apps); `SaveStrategyNotes` needs `DRAFT_STRATEGIES` or `strategies:manage`.
  All are main-account only.

## Migration

None: every field, message, RPC and error code is new. Existing clients see
two more fields on `Strategy` and keep working unchanged.
