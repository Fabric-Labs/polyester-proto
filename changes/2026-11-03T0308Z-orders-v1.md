## What and why

An order now says how much quote its fills used and what fee they paid, so a
client can tell from the order alone how much of a buy's quote amount was
used and how much was not. Until now an order sized by a quote amount
(`submitted_max_quote_debit_scaled`) showed only base quantities, which read
the same whether it spent its whole amount or stopped early at its price
limit, and it carried no fee amount.

`orders.v1.Order` has two new fields:

- `cum_notional` (`polyester.type.v1.U128`): the exact quote amount of the
  fills across the lineage through this generation, before fees. It is the
  sum over the fills of their quantity, scaled as `cum_qty_scaled`, times
  their price in quote units scaled by 1e9, so it is in quote units scaled by
  10^(9 + `base_quantity_scale`). Unset while the order has no fills. Every
  order read carries it, and so do private stream order updates.
- `fee_total` (`orders.v1.OrderFeeTotal`): the total fee of those fills, net
  of rebates, in the order's `fee_asset`: `amount_e18` (`U128`, fixed 18
  decimals), `is_rebate` (true when rebates exceed fees, so the amount was
  credited) and `qty_scaled` (the executed quantity of the fills it covers).
  A fill's fee is recorded shortly after the fill executes, so the total can
  trail the order's fills: it covers them all once `qty_scaled` equals
  `cum_qty_scaled`.
  Order history reads and single-order reads that include execution history
  (the default) carry it. Open-order reads, single-order reads with
  `include_execution_history` false, and private stream order updates leave
  it unset.

The REST order objects show them as `cumQuote`, a decimal string in quote
units, and `feeTotal` `{amount, isRebate, qty}` with decimal strings; open
orders show `cumQuote` only. No field, enum value or status changes. A buy
sized by a quote amount still ends `FILLED` when it spends its amount, and
`CANCELED` with `CANCEL_REASON_MARKET_REMAINDER` (market) or
`CANCEL_REASON_IOC_REMAINDER` (limit IOC) when the book or its price limit
stops it first.

## How clients use it

Read the order, then compute what a buy sized by a quote amount used:

- fee in quote (`fee_asset` `QUOTE`): used = `cumQuote` + `feeTotal.amount`,
  or `cumQuote` − `feeTotal.amount` when `feeTotal.isRebate` is true (a
  rebate is credited; a market buy always pays its fee);
- fee in base (`fee_asset` `BASE`, taken from the base received): used =
  `cumQuote`;
- unspent = `submittedMaxQuoteDebit` − used.

Use the fee total once `feeTotal.qty` equals `cumQty`; until then a fill's
fee is still being recorded, and a later read includes it.

REST:

```bash
curl -sS 'https://<api-host>/v1/spot/orders/<order-id>' -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>'
# {"order": {..., "status": "CANCELED", "cancelReason": "MARKET_REMAINDER",
#   "cumQty": "0.04148504", "submittedMaxQuoteDebit": "6000",
#   "cumQuote": "3215.5809531728", "feeAsset": "QUOTE",
#   "feeTotal": {"amount": "0.96467428595184", "isRebate": false, "qty": "0.04148504"}, ...}}
# used = 3215.5809531728 + 0.96467428595184 = 3216.54562745875184
# unspent = 6000 - 3216.54562745875184 = 2783.45437254124816
```

ConnectRPC, JSON:

```bash
curl -sS https://<api-host>/orders.v1.OrdersReadService/GetOrder \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>' \
  -d '{"orderId": "<order-id>"}'
# {"order": {..., "cumQtyScaled": "4148504", "submittedMaxQuoteDebitScaled": "6000000000",
#   "cumNotional": {"hi": "17", "lo": "7963446064217622528"},
#   "feeTotal": {"amountE18": {"lo": "964674285951840000"}, "qtyScaled": "4148504"}, ...}}
```

`cumNotional` is `hi × 2^64 + lo` = 321558095317280000000; at a
`base_quantity_scale` of 8 that is 321558095317280000000 / 10^17 =
3215.5809531728 quote. `amountE18` / 10^18 = 0.96467428595184. A zero word
and a false `isRebate` are omitted in JSON. `GET /v1/orders/history` and
`GetOrderHistory` show both fields the same way; `GET /v1/orders/open`,
`GetOpenOrders` and private stream order updates show `cumQuote` /
`cum_notional` only.

## Migration

None required: both fields are additive and no existing field changes. To
show how much of a buy's quote amount was used, read `cumQuote` and
`feeTotal` instead of estimating it from `avgPx` and `cumQty`.
