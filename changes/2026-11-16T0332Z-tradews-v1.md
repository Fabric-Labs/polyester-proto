## What and why

A trading WebSocket upgrade that opts in to cancel-on-disconnect is now
refused with `504` and `ORDER_CANCEL_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` as the response body
when the venue received the opt-in but did not confirm it in time. Before,
the same case was refused with `503` (`UNAVAILABLE` or
`ORDER_CANCEL_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`), which reads as "nothing happened, try
again".

That reading was wrong: the opt-in may have armed the account's
cancel-on-disconnect timer. If it did, the account's open orders are
cancelled once the grace period passes, unless a session of the account
opts in to cancel-on-disconnect again by then. A client that took the `503`
as a clean refusal and reconnected without the opt-in lost its orders with
no warning. `ORDER_CANCEL_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` is the code the Orders API
already answers when a `CancelAllAfter` is not confirmed in time.

`503` with `UNAVAILABLE` now means only that the opt-in was not sent or the
venue could not take it: nothing was armed, and retrying is safe. No message
or field changes.

## How clients use it

Opt in on the signed WebSocket upgrade request as before:

```text
X-Cancel-On-Disconnect: ACCOUNT
X-Cancel-On-Disconnect-Grace-Sec: 15
```

Read a refused upgrade's status and body code:

| Status | Body | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `504` | `ORDER_CANCEL_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` | The opt-in may have armed the timer. | Reconnect with the same opt-in within the grace period, then reconcile open orders. |
| `503` | `UNAVAILABLE` | Nothing was armed. | Retry the upgrade. |

After a `504`, only a new session with `X-Cancel-On-Disconnect: ACCOUNT`
keeps the account's orders. `CancelAllAfter` with a timeout of `0` does not
help: it disarms only the account's own cancel-all-after timer, which is
separate from the cancel-on-disconnect timer. If the client cannot reconnect
with the opt-in within the grace period, it must expect the account's open
orders to be cancelled with cancel reason `CANCEL_ON_DISCONNECT`, and read
them back before trading.

## Migration

1. Treat a refused cancel-on-disconnect upgrade with status `504` and body
   `ORDER_CANCEL_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` as "may be armed", never as a clean
   refusal.
2. On that answer, reconnect with the same opt-in within the grace period.
   Do not fall back to a session without the opt-in while the account has
   open orders you mean to keep.
3. Keep treating `503` `UNAVAILABLE` as a safe retry.
