## What and why

An order's `price_ticks` (REST `price`) now follows one documented rule. For
a LIMIT order it is the limit price. For a MARKET order it is the order's
protection price, the worst price it may fill at, whatever placed the order:
a direct market order, the market order of a stop-loss, take-profit, attached
risk leg or TWAP, and a trailing stop's market order. A BUY fills at or below
it and a SELL at or above it. It is the order's reference price moved by its
maximum slippage, or by the pair's default market slippage for its side (200
basis points if none is configured), on the pair's tick grid. A stop-loss's,
take-profit's or trailing stop's market order takes the price that fired it
as its reference.

The contract text said the field was zero for MARKET orders, but direct
market orders and stop-loss and take-profit market orders already returned
their protection price there. Only a trailing stop's market order with a
slippage cap returned zero, though its cap was applied. A reader could
conclude it ran without slippage protection. It now returns its protection
price like every other market order, and the text describes what every
order returns.

Order reads and private stream order updates carry the same value.

## How clients use it

Read a market order's protection price from its price. A trailing stop's
SELL market order capped at 150 bps that fired at 64,547.33 reads:

```http
GET /v1/spot/orders/{order_id}

{"order":{"orderId":"<order_id>","side":"SELL","orderType":"MARKET","timeInForce":"IOC","price":"63579.13","marketClientRefPrice":"64547.33","marketMaxSlippageBps":150}}
```

```http
POST /orders.v1.OrdersReadService/GetOrder
{"orderId":"<order_id>","includeExecutionHistory":false}

{"order":{"side":"SELL","orderType":"MARKET","priceTicks":"63579130000000","marketClientRefPriceTicks":"64547330000000","marketMaxSlippageBps":150}}
```

`GET /v1/orders/open` and `GET /v1/orders/history`, with their ConnectRPC
`GetOpenOrders` and `GetOrderHistory`, return the same price. Use
`orderType`, never a zero price, to tell a market order from a limit order.

## Migration

None required: the field, its type and its encoding are unchanged, and
direct, stop-loss and take-profit market orders already returned their
protection price. A client that treated a zero price as "market order" or
"no slippage protection" must read `orderType` and `marketMaxSlippageBps` /
`marketMaxSlippageTicks` instead. A trailing stop's market order with a
slippage cap now shows a non-zero price.
