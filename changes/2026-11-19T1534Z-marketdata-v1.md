## What and why

A spot pair's live price band can now be followed on a stream instead of
polling `GetPriceBand`. Two public channels per pair carry the same band
`GetPriceBand` returns:

- `public:spot:market:price_band:{symbolId}`: JSON, `GetPriceBand`'s REST
  body `{"band": {...}}`;
- `public:spot:market:price_band:{symbolId}:proto`: protobuf,
  `marketdata.v1.GetPriceBandResponse`.

`PriceBandState` gains `UNKNOWN = 4`. Only the streams send it: when no band
was confirmed within the last 5 seconds, a stream sends the band once with
state `UNKNOWN`, without prices, reference source or settings, and nothing
more until the band returns. Its `epoch`, `updateId` and `asOf` are those of
the last confirmation of the band it replaces, and its `updatedAt` and
`validUntil` equal `asOf`. `GetPriceBand` never returns it; it refuses with
`PRICE_BAND_STALE` or `PRICE_BAND_UNKNOWN` instead, which means the same.

## How clients use it

Subscribe anonymously to the pair's channel on the public WebSocket:

```text
public:spot:market:price_band:1
public:spot:market:price_band:1:proto
```

```json
{"band": {"symbol": "BTC-USDT", "state": "ACTIVE", "referencePrice": "61000.25",
  "referenceSource": "LAST_TRADE", "maxBuyPrice": "64050.25", "minSellPrice": "57950.25",
  "updatedAt": "2026-11-18T11:06:40.123456789Z", "reanchorEligibleAt": "2026-11-18T11:07:40.123456789Z",
  "reanchorPending": false, "parameters": {"lowerBps": 500, "upperBps": 500, "referenceWindowMs": 60000},
  "epoch": "3", "updateId": "41", "asOf": "2026-11-19T10:00:00.5Z", "validUntil": "2026-11-19T10:00:05.5Z"}}
```

- A changed band is sent at once; while the band stays current, an unchanged
  band is sent again about every 2 seconds, so `validUntil` stays ahead.
- Each message replaces the previous one. When a message is repeated or
  arrives late, keep the one with the greatest `epoch`, then `updateId`, then
  `asOf`; at an equal one, keep `UNKNOWN`. A later confirmation of the band
  replaces `UNKNOWN`.
- A band whose `validUntil` has passed is unknown, as is a band with state
  `UNKNOWN`.
- A new subscription receives no earlier message: read `GetPriceBand`, or,
  while the band stays current, wait about 2 seconds for the next one. After
  an `UNKNOWN` nothing is sent until the band returns. A reconnecting client
  recovers the messages it missed while the channel's history still holds
  them.

## Migration

None: the channels are new, and `GetPriceBand` never returns `UNKNOWN`. A
client that switches on `PriceBandState` should handle the new value when it
reads the streams.
