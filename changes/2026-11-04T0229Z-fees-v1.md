## What and why

`GetSpotFeeRates` now tells a client how much quote balance a buy order needs
for its fee, and how its effective rates are made up. Each `SpotFeeRate` gains:

- `reserve_fee_rate_percent` and `post_only_reserve_fee_rate_percent`: the
  rate an order reserves its fee at when the fee is paid in the quote asset.
  It is the highest rate of every VIP tier on the market under the fee
  schedule in force, maker only for a post-only limit order, so it can be
  above the account's effective rate. Fills charge the effective rate and
  release the rest of the reserve.
- `breakdown` (`SpotFeeRateBreakdown`): the account's VIP tier rates, the
  market's fee schedule multipliers (`SpotFeeMultiplier`), the account
  discounts and the referral discount in force (`SpotFeeDiscount`), each with
  the time it took effect and, when scheduled, the time it ends. The message
  documents the integer steps that reproduce the effective rates exactly.
- `next_change_at`: the earliest scheduled time at which a component starts or
  ends, when one is scheduled.

Order forms that size a buy from the effective maker or taker rate ask for
less fee than the order reserves, so the largest such order is refused for
insufficient funds.

## How clients use it

Size a buy limit order that pays its fee in the quote asset by the reserve
rate `r` (`reserve_fee_rate_percent`, or `post_only_reserve_fee_rate_percent`
for a post-only order). Work in units of 10^-18 of the quote asset, whatever
its own decimals:

```text
N = ceil(price × quantity)        rounded up to 10^-18 quote
F = ceil(N × r / 100)             rounded up to 10^-18 quote
the order has sufficient quote balance when N + F <= available quote balance
```

Other order validation and market limits still apply.

Do not round N or F to the quote asset's smallest unit: that asks for more
than the order reserves, so the largest order that fits looks refused. For
example, at a 0.15 reserve rate and a price of 1, 998,502.24663005 base
reserves 999,999.999999995075 and fits a 1,000,000 balance. A buy sized by a
quote amount reserves at the lower of the reserve rate and
`taker_fee_rate_percent`. Show the effective
rates as what fills are charged, and the breakdown to explain them. Fetch the
rates again at `next_change_at`.

```bash
curl -sS https://<api-host>/fees.v1.FeeService/GetSpotFeeRates \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"symbolId": [1]}'
```

```json
{"feeRates": [{"symbolId": 1, "makerFeeRatePercent": "0.072",
  "takerFeeRatePercent": "0.027", "vipTier": 1,
  "reserveFeeRatePercent": "0.09", "postOnlyReserveFeeRatePercent": "0.09",
  "breakdown": {"tierMakerFeeRatePercent": "0.08",
    "tierTakerFeeRatePercent": "0.135",
    "marketMultiplier": {"makerMultiplierPercent": "90",
      "takerMultiplierPercent": "20",
      "effectiveFrom": "2026-11-01T00:00:00Z"}}}]}
```

REST serves the same fields at `GET /v1/spot/fee-rates?symbol=BTC-USDT`, with
`symbol` instead of `symbolId` and times as RFC 3339 strings. Every rate is a
decimal percentage string; times are UTC.

## Migration

None: the fields are new and optional to read. Existing clients decode the
response as before.
