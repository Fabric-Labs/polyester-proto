## What and why

A deploy proposal now names the strategy and the revision it came from, so a
bot links back to the draft it was deployed from. A version can be the
content of several strategies' revisions, so a revision is the one precise
reference. Strategies can also be deleted.

- **Changed: `ProposeDeploy` takes `strategy_id` (7) and `revision` (8)
  instead of `version_id` (1), which is removed.** The proposed version is
  that revision's version. A strategy the caller doesn't have is
  `STRATEGY_NOT_FOUND`; a revision the strategy doesn't have, zero included,
  is `REVISION_NOT_FOUND`. Every other check is unchanged.
- **`DeployProposalSummary` gains `strategy_id` (9) and `revision` (10)**, so
  `ProposeDeploy` and `GetDeployProposal` show which draft a proposal came
  from. Both are zero once the strategy is deleted; `version_id` stays.
- **New `DeleteStrategy`**: deletes one of the caller's strategies and its
  revisions. It is refused with `STRATEGY_IN_USE` while:
  - an active deployment, one that hasn't stopped, was deployed from one of
    the strategy's proposals;
  - an active deployment runs the version of one of the strategy's
    revisions. This blocks the delete even when only that version links the
    deployment to the strategy and no proposal of the strategy is behind it;
  - a deploy proposal of the strategy is waiting: not expired, not failed
    and not yet deployed.

  A proposal whose deploy failed does not keep the strategy in use, even
  while it is unexpired. An unknown, already deleted or another account's
  strategy is `STRATEGY_NOT_FOUND`.
- **Removed: `StrategyDuplicateOrigin.source_strategy_id` (1).** A
  duplicate's origin names only its source version, `source_version_id`
  (2): a copy depends on the version, never on a strategy that saved it, so
  deleting that strategy never affects the copy. Copying a version that has
  expired and that nothing keeps any more is `VERSION_NOT_FOUND`.
- **New `ErrorCode` values:** `STRATEGY_IN_USE` (29) and
  `REVISION_NOT_FOUND` (30).
- **New messages:** `DeleteStrategyRequest` and `DeleteStrategyResponse`.

## How clients use it

```json
POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/ProposeDeploy
{"strategyId": "…", "revision": "3", "botName": "Weekly DCA",
 "funding": [{"asset": "USDT", "amount": "200"}], "idempotencyKey": "propose-1"}

{"proposalId": "…", "deployUrl": "https://<app-host>/bots/deploy/<proposal-id>",
 "summary": {"versionId": "sha256:…", "strategyId": "…", "revision": "3", …},
 "expiresAt": "…"}

POST /strategies.v1.StrategiesService/DeleteStrategy
{"strategyId": "…"}

{}
```

- `revision` is the number `SaveDraftVersion` returned, or a strategy's
  `revision` from `GetStrategy` or `ListStrategies`.
- `DeleteStrategy` needs `DRAFT_STRATEGIES` on an API key scoped to the main
  account. It has no REST route. Deleting never stops a bot: stop the bot
  with `StopBot` first, or let a waiting proposal expire.

## Migration

- `StrategyDuplicateOrigin`: stop reading `source_strategy_id`. Show a
  duplicate's origin from `source_version_id`.
- `ProposeDeploy`: stop sending `version_id`. Send the strategy's
  `strategy_id` and the `revision` to propose instead, as `SaveDraftVersion`
  or `GetStrategy` returned them. To propose a strategy's current draft,
  send its current `revision`.
