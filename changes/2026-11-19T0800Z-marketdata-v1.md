## What and why

`MarketDataService.GetPriceBand` returns a spot pair's live price band: the
reference price and the limit prices the pair admits now. Until now clients
saw only the band's settings in `GetSpotConfig` (`PairConfig.price_band`) and
had to estimate the limits from recent trades, so a limit order priced from
an old trade could be rejected `PRICE_BAND_VIOLATION`.

New messages and enums: `LivePriceBand`, `GetPriceBandRequest`,
`GetPriceBandResponse`, `PriceBandState` (`ACTIVE`, `OFF`, `EMPTY`) and
`PriceBandReferenceSource` (`LAST_TRADE`, `LISTING_REFERENCE`,
`INDEX_PRICE`). `LivePriceBand.parameters` reuses the existing `PriceBand`
settings message.

## How clients use it

ConnectRPC:

```http
POST /marketdata.v1.MarketDataService/GetPriceBand
Content-Type: application/json

{"symbolId": 1}
```

REST:

```http
GET /v1/spot/markets/BTC-USDT/price-band
```

```json
{"band": {"symbol": "BTC-USDT", "state": "ACTIVE", "referencePrice": "61000.25",
  "referenceSource": "LAST_TRADE", "maxBuyPrice": "64050.25", "minSellPrice": "57950.25",
  "updatedAt": "2026-11-18T11:06:40.123456789Z", "reanchorEligibleAt": "2026-11-18T11:07:40.123456789Z",
  "reanchorPending": false, "parameters": {"lowerBps": 500, "upperBps": 500, "referenceWindowMs": 60000},
  "epoch": "3", "updateId": "41", "asOf": "2026-11-19T09:30:00.250Z", "validUntil": "2026-11-19T09:30:05.250Z"}}
```

- Limits are inclusive and side-specific: check a buy limit price against
  `maxBuyPrice` (`max_buy_price_ticks`) and a sell against `minSellPrice`
  (`min_sell_price_ticks`). `minBuyPrice` and `maxSellPrice` apply only when
  present.
- Presence follows `state`. `ACTIVE` carries the reference and the limits;
  `EMPTY` carries the reference but no limits, and every order that adds
  exposure is rejected; `OFF` carries neither.
- The band is advisory: the pair checks every order on arrival, and the band
  may move first (`reanchorEligibleAt`, `reanchorPending`).
- Treat a band as unknown after `validUntil` (`asOf` + 5 s) and read it again.
- Order two bands by `epoch`, then `updateId` within one epoch.
- Errors: no band confirmed within 5 s fails `unavailable` / HTTP 503 with code
  `PRICE_BAND_STALE` (not confirmed in time) or `PRICE_BAND_UNKNOWN` (not
  known), REST adding `Retry-After: 1`. An unknown pair fails `not_found` /
  404 `UNKNOWN_SYMBOL`. Responses are never cached.
- Prices are int64 ticks scaled by 1e9 on ConnectRPC and decimal strings on
  REST; times are nanoseconds since the Unix epoch on ConnectRPC and RFC 3339
  on REST.

## Migration

None: the RPC and its messages are new.
