## What and why

A batch of internal transfers can now be read back, and every leg of a
batch appears in history and has its own lifecycle flow, like a single
internal transfer.

- `transfer.v1.InternalTransferService.GetInternalTransferBatch` reads a
  batch's current outcome and the state of every leg by the `request_id`
  that `BatchCreateInternalTransfers` returned. It answers exactly as the
  batch was answered, so a client that received `BATCH_PROCESSING` can
  follow the batch without resubmitting it. The read does not submit or
  retry the batch and needs no fresh MFA.
- `InternalTransferBatchLegResult.flow_id` returns at its old field 5,
  with its old type (string). It names the leg's lifecycle flow once the
  leg is credited, in both the create and the read answers.
- `ledger.v1.TransferCode.INTERNAL_BATCH` (1078) is a leg of a batch. Each
  credited leg appears in `ledger.read.v1.LedgerReadService.ListTransfers`
  for its sender (debit) and its recipient (credit), under the leg's
  `transfer_id` and `flow_id`. Legs of one batch share its batch reference.

## How clients use it

ConnectRPC:

```http
POST https://<api-host>/transfer.v1.InternalTransferService/GetInternalTransferBatch
Content-Type: application/json

{"requestId": "92640625967207661803151833376978068587"}
```

REST:

```http
GET https://<api-host>/v1/trading/internal-transfers/batch?requestId=92640625967207661803151833376978068587
```

The answer has the same shape as `BatchCreateInternalTransfers`: `requestId`,
`status` and `legs`. A credited leg carries `flowId`:

```json
{
  "requestId": "92640625967207661803151833376978068587",
  "status": "ACCEPTED",
  "legs": [
    {"legIndex": 0, "status": "CREDITED", "transferId": "<transfer id>", "flowId": "flow_<id>", "creditedAtTsNs": "1763330000000000000", "assetId": 7, "assetCode": "BTC", "amount": "0.5"}
  ]
}
```

- Sessions and API keys can read a batch. The caller must be allowed to
  read the transfers of every source account of the batch; any other batch
  is answered `NOT_FOUND`, as an unknown one is.
- Read a leg's flow with `GetAccountFlow` (`GET /v1/chain/account-flows/{flow_id}`)
  as its sender or its recipient.
- Filter history for batch legs with `transferCode=INTERNAL_BATCH` in
  `ListTransfers`. History shows a leg only once it is credited.

## Migration

None. The RPC and the enum value are new. `flow_id` comes back at the
number and type it had before the batch became all or nothing, so a client
built on either contract decodes it. A client that lists transfers and
switches on `TransferCode` should handle `INTERNAL_BATCH`.
