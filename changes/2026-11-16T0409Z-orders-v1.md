## What and why

`PreviewOrder` now checks a limit order's price against the market's price
band, as `CreateOrder` does. Before, a preview could answer
`admissible: true` for a limit order that the band then rejected
`PRICE_BAND_VIOLATION` right after it was created.

A limit price past the band now previews `admissible: false` with
`rejection.code` `PRICE_BAND_VIOLATION`, and `orders.v1.ErrorDetail` has one
new field:

- `price_band_limit_ticks` (optional `int64`): the band limit the order's
  limit price is past, the highest admitted buy price for a buy, the lowest
  admitted sell price for a sell, as the market last published its band.
  Quote units scaled by 1e9. Set only with `PRICE_BAND_VIOLATION` on a
  preview; omitted for every other error.

The band limits each side separately: a buy limit may not be above the
highest buy price and a sell limit may not be below the lowest sell price,
both edges admitted; a buy below the price or a sell above it is not limited.
A market order's price is not checked against these limits: its execution
stays within the band. A market whose band is unusable (no price fits it)
refuses every order that adds exposure, market orders included, at
`CreateOrder`; the preview does not report that case. The check uses the
band the market last published; when the preview has no current band, it
skips the band check. The preview stays advisory: `CreateOrder` checks the
band again at admission.

## How clients use it

Call `PreviewOrder` before `CreateOrder`. When `admissible` is false and the
code is `PRICE_BAND_VIOLATION`, show the limit: the order would be accepted
at or below it for a buy, at or above it for a sell.

```http
POST /orders.v1.OrdersService/PreviewOrder
{"order":{"symbolId":1,"side":"SIDE_BUY","baseQtyScaled":"2565000","limitGtc":{"priceTicks":"1950000000000"}}}

200 OK
{"admissible":false,"rejection":{"code":"ERROR_CODE_PRICE_BAND_VIOLATION","priceBandLimitTicks":"1837500000000"},
 "resolvedBaseQtyScaled":"2565000","protectedPriceBoundTicks":"1950000000000","evaluatedAt":"2026-11-15T21:58:04.120Z"}
```

```http
POST /v1/orders/preview
{"order":{"symbol":"ETH-USDT","side":"BUY","baseQty":"0.002565","limitGtc":{"price":"1950"}}}

200 OK
{"admissible":false,"rejection":{"code":"PRICE_BAND_VIOLATION","priceBandLimit":"1837.5"},
 "resolvedBaseQty":"0.002565","protectedPriceBound":"1950","evaluatedAt":"2026-11-15T21:58:04.120Z"}
```

## Migration

None: the field is new and optional, and a preview that was admissible
inside the band stays admissible. Clients that branch on `admissible` now see
`false` for a limit price past the band, which `CreateOrder` would have
rejected.
