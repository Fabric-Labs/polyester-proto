## What and why

**Availability:** devnet only. Not available on testnet yet; the service isn't deployed or routed there.

The assistant's transfers table now carries each leg's `link_id`, as the
ledger read API's `ListTransfers` returns it. Legs the ledger links to each
other, such as the base, quote and fee legs of one trade, share one non-zero
`link_id`, so a client can show a trade as one entry ("Bought 0.0005 BTC for
37.63 USDT, fee 0.0113 USDT") instead of three unrelated rows.

- `TransfersTable.transfers` (`repeated ledger.read.v1.TransferRow`) sets
  `TransferRow.link_id`. Its comment no longer says that `link_id` is not set.
- No field, message or enum is added or changed.

## How clients use it

Watch a run as before; the transfers table arrives as a `view` event after the
assistant lists transfers:

```text
POST /ai.v1.AssistantService/WatchRun
{"runId": "6349022007070785130", "afterSeq": "0"}

{"event": {"seq": "3", "view": {"transfersTable": {"transfers": [
  {"assetId": 1, "transferCode": "TAKER_FEE", "isDebit": true, "linkId": "23049001082519", "transferId": "485115054523517659064176890411266209659", "…": "…"},
  {"assetId": 1, "transferCode": "TRADE_QUOTE", "isDebit": true, "linkId": "23049001082519", "transferId": "664966600396164970858959830444458100660", "…": "…"},
  {"assetId": 3, "transferCode": "TRADE_BASE", "isDebit": false, "linkId": "23049001082519", "transferId": "351866130970509120690127410441096400104", "…": "…"},
  {"assetId": 1, "transferCode": "FUNDING_TO_TRADING", "isDebit": false, "transferId": "37831832577218654362892809825451311742", "…": "…"}
]}}}}
```

- Group rows by `link_id` only when it is non-zero. Zero (omitted in JSON)
  means the ledger linked the leg to nothing, for example a move between the
  funding and trading buckets; never group such rows with each other.
- `link_id` is a `uint64`, a decimal string in JSON.
- The table holds at most 100 legs, so a linked group can be cut off at the
  table's end when `truncated` is true.

## Migration

None: the field already existed and was always zero in this view. A client
that ignores `link_id` keeps working unchanged.
