## What and why

Swap now converts between any two assets that spot markets connect, and
can spread a conversion over time.

- **Two-leg routes.** When no market trades the two assets directly, a Swap
  routes through one intermediate asset (for example BTC to SOL through
  USDT), choosing the route with the largest expected output. Each leg pays
  the account's own spot taker fee on its market, in the asset the leg
  receives. No other fee is charged.
- **TWAP execution.** `SwapTerms.execution` chooses `market` (the default
  when unset) or `twap`, a duration of 1 to 60 minutes in slices at least
  10 seconds apart. Each slice nets at least the Swap's minimum rate
  (`min_amount_out_e18` over `amount_e18`) on what it converts, judged slice
  by slice. On a two-leg route, each slice's second leg converts that slice's
  intermediate, together with the intermediate of earlier slices for which
  no valid second-leg order was possible, which it carries.
- **Fee protection.** Each order's price limit allows for the taker fee in
  force when the order can match within its window, a few minutes past its
  sending window. An order the exchange first accepts after that window
  (only under severe delay) may pay a later scheduled fee; if that takes a
  fill below the minimum rate, the Swap fails closed with
  `MINIMUM_NOT_MET`.
- **Routes.** An intermediate asset is USDT or USDC only.
- **Remainders stay on the account.** Untraded source amount, and
  intermediate the second market cannot take in one order, stay on the
  account. The second is reported with the new reason
  `INTERMEDIATE_BELOW_MINIMUM` and in `intermediate_remainder_e18`.

Added:

- `MarketExecution`, `TwapExecution` and `Execution`, with the same shape as
  QuickSwap's but a 60-minute maximum duration.
- `SwapTerms.execution`.
- `SwapQuote.intermediate_asset_id`, `price_impact_bps` (end to end over
  every leg) and `total_slices`.
- `Swap.execution`, `intermediate_asset_id`, `intermediate_remainder_e18`,
  `legs` (`SwapLeg`: per-leg settled spend, receipt, fee and fee asset) and
  `progress` (`SwapProgress`, with up to 50 `SwapSlice` entries).
- `SwapOrder.slice`.
- `SwapReason.INTERMEDIATE_BELOW_MINIMUM`.

Removed: `Swap.fee_e18` (field 14). It reported one total fee in the
destination asset, which a two-leg Swap cannot express: its first leg's fee
is charged in the intermediate asset.

## How clients use it

Quote and create a 30-minute TWAP of 0.5 BTC into SOL:

```json
POST /swap.v1.SwapService/QuoteSwap
{"terms": {"sourceAssetId": 1, "destinationAssetId": 5,
  "amountE18": {"lo": "500000000000000000"},
  "execution": {"twap": {"durationMs": "1800000", "sliceIntervalMs": "60000"}}},
 "maxSlippageBps": 100}
```

The quote lists both legs, `intermediateAssetId` and `totalSlices: 30`.
Create with the same terms, the quote's `minAmountOutE18` and `expiresAt` as
`quoteExpiresAt`. The execution is part of the idempotent terms: the same
key with another execution is `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`. Follow the Swap with
`GetSwap` until `isTerminal`: `progress.slices` shows each slice (the first
50; `progress.slicesTruncated` says when more exist, and
`progress.completedSlices` still counts them all), and `legs` shows each
leg's totals. A TWAP's second legs may finish after their slice's slot, up
to the deadline.

An amount too small for every slice to be a valid order is
`AMOUNT_OUT_OF_RANGE`, with `min_amount_e18` the least amount that quotes for
that schedule.

CreateSwap now refuses idempotency keys starting `aw:` (INVALID_ARGUMENT):
they are reserved for auto-withdraw's conversions, which are Swaps of the
account too.

## Migration

Clients that read `Swap.fee_e18` must read the per-leg fees instead: sum
`Swap.legs[].fee_e18` per `fee_asset_id`. A direct Swap has one leg, whose
fee is in the destination asset, as before. Nothing else changes for
existing requests: an unset `execution` is a market Swap, and direct routes
quote as before.
