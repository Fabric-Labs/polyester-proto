## What and why

`Subaccount` gains `strategy_bot`, which is true once a strategy bot has held
the sub-account. It is a historical mark. It is never cleared, also after the
bot's access ends or the sub-account's policy is cleared, so it does not say
which policy the sub-account has now. Clients can use it to keep bot
sub-accounts out of funding-source choices without guessing from a label or
policy.

`ListSubaccounts`, `GetSubaccount` and `UpdateSubaccount` answer it, and
real-time sub-account updates carry it too. It is read-only.

## How clients use it

```json
// GET /v1/auth/subaccounts
// (POST /auth.v1.SubaccountService/ListSubaccounts)
{"subaccounts": [{"id": "1234567890", "label": "Weekly DCA", "strategyBot": true}]}
```

Leave a sub-account with `strategyBot` out of the sub-accounts offered as a
funding source for a new bot. A sub-account without it reads `false`, and JSON
omits the field. Read the sub-account's current policy from
`subaccountPolicyId`, not from this field.

## Migration

None: the field is new and optional, and existing requests and responses are
unchanged.
