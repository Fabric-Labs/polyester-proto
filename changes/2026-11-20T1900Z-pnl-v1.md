## What and why

Profit and loss now reports what a return needs, so clients can show ROI, maximum drawdown and a Sharpe ratio without computing them, and every client shows the same figures:

- each `DailyProfitAndLoss` day gains `start_equity_usd`, `end_equity_usd` and `net_flow_usd`: the account's equity at the 00:00 UTC that starts and ends the day, and its deposits and transfers in minus withdrawals and transfers out, each valued when it settled;
- `GetTradingStatisticsResponse` gains `return_pct` (time-weighted return), `max_drawdown_pct`, `sharpe` and `return_days`;
- `ProfitAndLossNotice` gains `EQUITY_UNAVAILABLE`, `FLOW_UNVALUED` and `SHARPE_INSUFFICIENT_DAYS`.

Equity is everything the account owns, funds reserved by open orders and pending withdrawals included, valued at the 00:00 UTC prices. A deposit or withdrawal is not a gain or loss: a day's return is `(end - start - flow) / (start + flow / 2)`, and a day that starts with no equity starts at its first deposit, `(end - flow) / flow`. The daily returns are compounded into `return_pct`.

## How clients use it

ConnectRPC `POST /pnl.v1.ProfitAndLossService/GetTradingStatistics`:

```json
{ "window": "PROFIT_AND_LOSS_WINDOW_DAYS_30" }
```

REST `GET /v1/pnl/statistics?window=DAYS_30` answers, for example:

```json
{ "returnPct": "9.95", "maxDrawdownPct": "0", "returnDays": 2, "notices": ["SHARPE_INSUFFICIENT_DAYS"] }
```

- The return figures use closed UTC days only. `DAYS_7` and `DAYS_30` are the last 7 and 30 closed days, `LIFETIME` every closed day, and `TODAY` none. Today's partial day never counts. The trade count, volume, fees and realized PnL keep their window, today included.
- The return figures cover the whole account, whatever `symbol_id` and `product` select.
- Percentages are percent strings: `"1.25"` is 1.25%. `sharpe` is a plain ratio.
- `sharpe` needs at least 7 daily returns. Below that it is omitted with `SHARPE_INSUFFICIENT_DAYS`; show "based on `return_days` days".
- A figure that needs a missing price is omitted, never 0, with `EQUITY_UNAVAILABLE` or `FLOW_UNVALUED`. So is a figure after a day with `UNCLASSIFIED_TRANSFER` or `QUANTITY_SHORTFALL`.
- `ListDailyProfitAndLoss` (`GET /v1/pnl/daily`) returns the three new day fields. `end_equity_usd` is omitted for today until the day closes.

## Migration

None: every field and notice is new and optional. Clients that compute a return from PnL should switch to `return_pct`, which keeps deposits and withdrawals out of the return.
