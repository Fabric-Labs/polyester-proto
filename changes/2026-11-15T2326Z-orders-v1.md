## What and why

Documentation only: error-code and field descriptions now state what clients
observe. No field, enum value, RPC or wire behavior changed.

- `orders.v1.ErrorCode`: `STALE_QUOTE` names the market price it compares
  with and `max_client_ref_drift_bps` from GetSpotConfig; `API_KEY_UNKNOWN`
  also covers a key whose policy cannot be found; `CANCEL_REQUEST_EXPIRED`
  applies to a cancel-all request older than its two-minute window;
  `PRICE_BAND_VIOLATION`, `POLICY_MARKET_DENY`, `TRIGGER_PRICE_INVALID` and
  `UPSTREAM_ERROR` describe the observable cause. `MARKET_CAP_VIOLATION` and
  `GATEWAY_MISCONFIGURED` are marked as not currently returned.
  `ATTACHED_RISK_NOT_SUPPORTED` now covers only legs on an order placed by a
  triggered take-profit, stop-loss or trailing-stop leg: attached trailing
  stops, and leg changes together with a price, quantity or client order ID
  change or a replace, are accepted.
- `orders.v1.LimitGtc.post_only`: post-only is available on GTC and GTD limit
  orders.
- `orders.v1.Order.market_max_slippage_ticks`: zero when the order carries no
  ticks cap, for example because it set `market_max_slippage_bps`.
- `auth.v1` `require_member_mfa` on a sub-account invite: accepting requires an
  enrolled MFA factor and a recently MFA-elevated session.
  `AUTH_INTERNAL_ERROR` reads as an unexpected server error.
- `chain.withdraw.v1.ErrorCode` and `transfer.v1.ErrorCode`:
  `CAPITAL_VIEW_UNAVAILABLE` means the available balance cannot be confirmed
  right now; withdraw `UNSUPPORTED_LEDGER` means `asset_id` does not identify
  a known asset.
- `ledger.read.v1.ErrorCode.UPSTREAM_ERROR`: ledger data is temporarily
  unavailable.

The REST problem `detail` texts of the same codes, and the realtime candle
stream's documented JSON schema (`quoteVolume`, always present) and Protobuf
example scales, were aligned too.

## How clients use it

Nothing changes in how clients call the APIs. For example, a market order
whose client reference price drifts too far still fails with
`ERROR_CODE_STALE_QUOTE`:

```json
POST /orders.v1.OrdersService/CreateOrder
{"order": {"symbolId": 1, "side": "BUY", "baseQtyScaled": "100000000",
           "marketIoc": {"clientRefPriceTicks": "101250000000"}}}
```

Handle `ERROR_CODE_STALE_QUOTE` by re-reading the market price and retrying
with a current reference, or by omitting the client reference price.
